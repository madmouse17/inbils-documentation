The **Akun** (Account) tab shows your **Nama** (Name), **Email** and **Telepon** (Phone) ("Belum diisi" (Not filled in) if empty), the **Tampilan** (Appearance) choice, and the menu below them.

![The Akun tab with the Tampilan choice](~/assets/guide/technician/redesign/akun-teal-dark.webp)

**Tampilan** chooses the colour mode of the app: **Sistem** (System) (follows the phone settings), **Terang** (Light) or **Gelap** (Dark). The choice applies at once and is saved on this phone. The main colour follows the ISP brand colour, in both light and dark mode.

Menu: **Ubah profil** (Edit profile), **Ganti kata sandi** (Change password), **Perangkat** (Devices), **Notifikasi uji** (Test notification), **Tentang aplikasi** (About the app), **Keluar** (Sign out). This menu can still be opened without internet, so you can still sign out of the account. **Notifikasi uji** itself needs internet to send its message.


## Edit profile


![The Ubah profil screen](~/assets/guide/technician/ubah-profil.webp)

1. Tap **Ubah profil**.
2. Change **Nama** (required) and **Telepon**.
3. Tap **Simpan** (Save). The app writes "Profil diperbarui." (Profile updated.)

**Email login** (Login email) is shown but cannot be changed in the app. Contact the Inbils office if it needs to be changed. Common error: **"Isi nama Anda." (Fill in your name.)** if the name is empty.


## Change password


![The Ganti kata sandi screen](~/assets/guide/technician/ganti-kata-sandi.webp)

1. Tap **Ganti kata sandi**.
2. Fill in **Kata sandi saat ini** (Current password), **Kata sandi baru** (New password) and **Ulangi kata sandi baru** (Repeat the new password).
3. Tap **Ganti kata sandi**.

After success, **all devices that are signed in are signed out, including this phone**. The app opens the **Masuk** (Sign in) screen with the message "Kata sandi sudah diganti. Masuk lagi dengan kata sandi baru." (The password has been changed. Sign in again with the new password.) Sign in again with the new password. The same password applies on the website.

Common errors:

- **"Kata sandi saat ini salah." (The current password is wrong.)** Check the old password.
- **"Kata sandi baru minimal 8 karakter." (The new password must be at least 8 characters.)**
- **"Ulangan kata sandi baru tidak sama." (The repeated new password does not match.)**
- **"Terlalu banyak percobaan." (Too many attempts.)** You entered the old password wrongly too often. Wait for the countdown.


## Devices


![The Perangkat screen](~/assets/guide/technician/perangkat.webp)

The **Perangkat** screen lists the phones that are signed in to your account, with the phone name and the time it was last used. The phone you are using is labelled **Perangkat ini** (This device).

To sign out a phone (for example a lost phone):

1. Tap the sign-out icon on the row of that phone.
2. Confirm "Keluarkan perangkat?" (Sign out the device?).
3. That phone is signed out of your account and must sign in again. The app writes "Perangkat dikeluarkan." (Device signed out.)

If you sign out the phone you are using, you are also signed out of the app. Pull the screen down to refresh the list.


## About the app


![The Tentang aplikasi screen](~/assets/guide/technician/tentang-aplikasi.webp)

It shows **Aplikasi** (App), **Versi** (Version) and the build number, **ID aplikasi** (App ID), **Lingkungan** (Environment), **Server**, and **Lisensi sumber terbuka** (Open-source licences). Quote this version and build when you report a problem to the office. This screen can also be opened from the **Masuk** screen before you sign in:

## Signing out


1. Tap **Keluar**.
2. Confirm "Keluar dari akun?" (Sign out of the account?). You need to sign in again to use the app.
3. If photos are still in the upload queue or actions have not been sent yet, the dialogue writes "N bukti atau aksi belum terkirim akan dihapus. Keluar?" (N evidence files or actions not yet sent will be deleted. Sign out?). All of them are deleted from the phone if you continue. Cancel and send them first if needed.
