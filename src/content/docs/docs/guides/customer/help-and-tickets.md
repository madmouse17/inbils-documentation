title: "6. Help and tickets"
description: "Report a problem to your ISP: try the quick steps, create a ticket with photos, follow replies, rate the result and reopen a ticket if needed."
---

The **Bantuan** (Help) tab is for reporting a problem or asking your ISP for help. It contains an outage card from the ISP (when there is one), the **Ada masalah?** (Having a problem?) shortcut, your ticket list, and the ISP contacts. Every report is recorded as a ticket with a number and staff can reply to it. There is only one way in to report a problem and it is always called **Lapor masalah** (Report a problem): problem type first, then quick steps, then the form.


## Ticket list


![Bantuan tab with an outage card at the top, the Ada masalah? card, and the ticket list](~/assets/guide/customer/bantuan-gangguan.webp)

The screen shows, from top to bottom:

1. **Outage card** when the ISP publishes an announcement of type Gangguan (Outage) for your area or package: the announcement title, "Ada gangguan di area Anda sejak 19.40." (There is an outage in your area since 19.40.) and "Perkiraan pulih 22.00." (Estimated recovery 22.00.) (the estimate only appears when the ISP has filled it in and the time has not passed). Tap **Ikuti pembaruan** (Follow updates) to read the announcement. The same card appears on the **Ada masalah?** screen, so you know the cause before you create a second ticket.
2. The **Ada masalah?** card ("Coba langkah cepat dulu sebelum melapor masalah." (Try the quick steps first before reporting a problem.)). Tap it to open the problem types (next section).
3. **Tiket Anda** (Your tickets) with the filter **Aktif** (Active) (tickets that are Terbuka (Open), Ditugaskan (Assigned), and Dikerjakan (In progress)) or **Selesai** (Completed) (tickets that are Diselesaikan (Resolved) and Ditutup (Closed)).
4. Ticket cards. Each card shows the ticket code, the title, the status, the "Diperbarui" (Updated) date, and three progress stages: **Dibuka** (Opened), **Ditangani** (Being handled), and **Selesai** (Completed). The **Ditangani** stage lights up when the ticket is Ditugaskan or Dikerjakan, and the **Selesai** stage when the ticket is Diselesaikan or Ditutup. A screen reader says "Tahap 2 dari 3: Ditangani" (Stage 2 of 3: Being handled). The copy icon at the top right of the card copies the ticket code ("Nomor tiket disalin." (Ticket number copied.)). Give that code when you contact the ISP by phone or WhatsApp. Tap the card to open the details.
5. Scroll down to load the next tickets, then the **Hubungi {nama ISP}** (Contact {ISP name}) card with WhatsApp, phone, email, website, and the service hours filled in by the ISP (this part is the same as the card on the **Akun** (Account) tab, [chapter 8](/docs/guides/customer/account/)).

The **Lapor masalah** button floats at the bottom right and opens the same screen as the **Ada masalah?** card. Pull the screen down to refresh the list and the outage card.

When empty: "Tidak ada tiket aktif." (No active tickets.) or "Belum ada tiket selesai." (No completed tickets yet.)

Ticket statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Terbuka (Open) | The ticket was received and is waiting to be handled. |
| Ditugaskan (Assigned) | The ticket has been handed to a staff member. |
| Dikerjakan (In progress) | Staff are handling it. |
| Diselesaikan (Resolved) | Staff state that the problem is solved. The ticket waits for your confirmation (section "Masalah sudah beres?" (Is the problem solved?)). |
| Ditutup (Closed) | The ticket is closed and accepts no new replies or attachments. When the problem is still there, report again with the **Buat tiket baru** (Create a new ticket) button on that ticket. |


## Having a problem? Try the quick steps first


Before you send a report, the app offers steps you can try yourself. Many connection problems are solved with these steps.

![Ada masalah?: choose the problem type](~/assets/guide/customer/redesign/ada-masalah-kategori-teal-dark.webp)

1. On the **Bantuan** tab, tap the **Ada masalah?** card or the **Lapor masalah** button. From Home, tap **Lapor masalah** in the quick actions. The screen says "Pilih jenis masalah yang Anda alami." (Choose the type of problem you have.)
2. Tap a category, for example **No Internet** or **Slow Connection**. The list of categories follows what the ISP has set up.
3. Read the **Coba dulu** (Try first) card. When the ISP has written **Saran untuk pelanggan** (Advice for customers) for that category, the card shows the ISP's text as it is, including its line breaks. When the ISP has not written any, the card shows three general steps:
   - Check the modem lights. Note any light that is off or red.
   - Unplug the modem power cable, wait 30 seconds, then plug it in again.
   - Check the cables on the modem. Make sure none is loose, bent, or broken.
4. When you have more than one service that can be reported, choose **Layanan yang bermasalah** (Service with the problem) (or leave "Tidak terkait layanan" (Not related to a service)). When there is only one, the app chooses it by itself.
5. When the problem has not gone away, tap **Masih bermasalah? Lapor masalah** (Still having a problem? Report it). The form opens with the category and service already filled in.

![Ada masalah?: general steps in the Coba dulu card and the Masih bermasalah? Lapor masalah button](~/assets/guide/customer/ada-masalah-langkah-cepat.webp)

This screen only gives advice. Nothing is sent to the ISP until you tap **Kirim tiket** (Send ticket). When the ISP has not set up categories, the screen says "Belum ada kategori masalah. Tarik layar ke bawah untuk memuat ulang." (There are no problem categories yet. Pull the screen down to refresh.) Contact the ISP through the contact card on the **Bantuan** tab.


## Report a problem


![Lapor masalah form with Kategori and Layanan filled in, and the same active ticket card](~/assets/guide/customer/tiket-baru.webp)

1. Tap **Masih bermasalah? Lapor masalah** on the **Ada masalah?** screen. The **Lapor masalah** form opens with **Kategori** (Category) and **Layanan** (Service) already filled in.
2. Choose the **Kategori**. Required. Once a category is chosen, **Judul** (Title) is filled with the category name ("No Internet", for example). Change the title when needed. A title you wrote yourself is not overwritten when you change the category.
3. Choose **Layanan (opsional)** (Service (optional)) when the problem relates to one subscription. The choices only list services that are active or suspended. When there is only one service, the app chooses it by itself. Leave "Tidak terkait layanan" when there is none.
4. Fill in or change the **Judul**, required, at most 255 characters.
5. Fill in **Deskripsi (opsional)** (Description (optional)): what happened, since when, which lights are on at the device. Below the field there are five quick answers: "Gangguan sejak hari ini." (Outage since today.), "Gangguan sejak kemarin." (Outage since yesterday.), "Lampu LOS di modem menyala merah." (The LOS light on the modem is red.), "Lampu modem normal." (The modem lights are normal.) and "Modem sudah dinyalakan ulang." (The modem has been restarted.) Tap one to add it as a line in the description, tap again to remove it. You can add your own sentences around them.
6. To add photos, tap **Tambah lampiran** (Add attachment) (see below). You can add more than one.
7. Tap **Kirim tiket**. This button stays visible above the keyboard.

**Duplicate ticket warning.** When you already have an active ticket with the same category (and the same service, or one of them without a service), a yellow card below the choice fields says "Anda sudah punya tiket aktif TKT-0031 (Terbuka)." (You already have an active ticket TKT-0031 (Open).) with two buttons: **Lihat tiket** (View ticket) opens that ticket, **Tetap buat baru** (Create a new one anyway) hides the card. This card is only a suggestion; **Kirim tiket** can still be tapped.

The ticket is sent, then the app opens its details page. The back button from that page takes you to the ticket list, not to the form. The system sets the priority according to the category. Photos are uploaded after the ticket is created; when some fail, the app shows "Tiket terkirim, tetapi N lampiran gagal diunggah. Tambahkan lagi dari halaman tiket." (The ticket was sent, but N attachments failed to upload. Add them again from the ticket page.)

When Kategori or Judul is empty, the app shows "Pilih kategori." (Choose a category.) or "Isi judul tiket." (Fill in the ticket title.) and does not send. When the category list fails to load, it says "Kategori tidak bisa dimuat." (Categories could not be loaded.); try again once the connection is back.

When the ISP has enabled WhatsApp for you, a "ticket created" message is sent after the ticket is recorded.


## Ticket details


![Ticket details](~/assets/guide/customer/detail-tiket.webp)

The screen shows a title card with the status, the progress stages (**Dibuka**, **Ditangani**, **Selesai**), the category, and the related service. Below the divider there is a summary:

- **Prioritas** (Priority) ("Prioritas sedang" (Medium priority)), "Dibuat {tanggal}" (Created {date}), and "Dibuka kembali {tanggal}" (Reopened {date}) when the ticket has been reopened.
- The ISP's promise for the first answer: "Dijawab sebelum 08.30 besok" (Answered before 08.30 tomorrow), with the counter "sisa 17 jam" (17 hours left) (yellow when under 4 hours, red with "terlambat 2 jam" (2 hours late) when past). After the answer it says "Dijawab {tanggal}" (Answered {date}).
- The resolution promise: "Diselesaikan sebelum Sen 17.00" (Resolved before Mon 17.00), with the same counter. After completion it says "Diselesaikan {tanggal}" (Resolved {date}).
- "Ditangani oleh {nama depan petugas}" (Handled by {staff first name}).

![Ticket details with the answer and resolution deadlines](~/assets/guide/customer/detail-tiket-tenggat.webp)

A deadline that is still ahead is written the way people say it: "21.17" for today, "08.30 besok" (tomorrow) for tomorrow, "Sen 08.30" (Mon 08.30) for two to six days ahead, and "18 Okt 08.30" (18 Oct 08.30) for further away. A deadline that has passed is written with the full date. Then come **Deskripsi** (Description), **Catatan penyelesaian** (Resolution notes) (when staff fill it in), the confirmation card (when there is one), and **Balasan** (Replies). The copy icon in the top bar copies the ticket code. Pull down to refresh.

A **Ditutup** ticket replaces the reply box with the card **Tiket ditutup. Masih bermasalah? Buat tiket baru** (Ticket closed. Still having a problem? Create a new ticket) and the **Buat tiket baru** button. The button opens the **Lapor masalah** form with the category and service of the old ticket already filled in. A closed ticket accepts no new replies or attachments.


## Is the problem solved?


When staff mark the ticket **Diselesaikan**, the ticket details show the **Masalah sudah beres?** card ("Beri nilai untuk menutup tiket ini. Kalau belum beres, balas tiket ini dan kami buka kembali." (Give a rating to close this ticket. If it is not solved, reply to this ticket and we will reopen it.)) with two buttons.

![Resolved ticket details with the Masalah sudah beres? card](~/assets/guide/customer/detail-tiket-sudah-beres.webp)

1. Tap **Sudah beres** (Solved) when your problem is solved. The **Beri nilai** (Give a rating) sheet opens: "Seberapa puas Anda dengan penanganan tiket ini?" (How satisfied are you with how this ticket was handled?)
2. Tap 1 to 5 stars. Below the stars, the meaning is written: Sangat buruk (Very bad), Kurang (Poor), Cukup (Fair), Baik (Good), or Sangat baik (Very good). Without stars, it says "Pilih jumlah bintang." (Choose the number of stars.)
3. If you want, write in **Ceritakan pengalaman Anda (opsional)** (Tell us about your experience (optional)).
4. Tap **Kirim nilai** (Send rating). The app says "Terima kasih. Tiket ditutup." (Thank you. The ticket is closed.) and the ticket becomes **Ditutup**.

![Beri nilai sheet with five stars and the experience field](~/assets/guide/customer/beri-nilai.webp)

Tap **Masih bermasalah** (Still a problem) when it is not solved: the reply field at the bottom of the screen becomes active at once. Your reply reopens the ticket (next section) and your rating is not asked for.

A ticket that was closed without you rating it shows the card "Bagaimana penanganan tiket ini?" (How was this ticket handled?) with a **Beri nilai** button. After rating, the card changes into **Penilaian Anda** (Your rating): your stars and comment. A ticket is rated once. When the ticket has already been rated from elsewhere, the app says "Tiket ini sudah dinilai." (This ticket has already been rated.), and when staff reopened the ticket first, "Tiket ini belum diselesaikan, jadi belum bisa dinilai." (This ticket is not resolved, so it cannot be rated yet.)


## Replies, photos, and PDFs


Text and photo replies use one box at the bottom of the screen and appear in one timeline above it.

1. Open the details of a ticket that is not **Ditutup**. The box at the bottom has a paperclip icon (**Tambah lampiran**), the **Balasan Anda** (Your reply) field, and a send icon (**Kirim balasan** (Send reply)). The box stays visible above the keyboard.
2. A **Diselesaikan** ticket shows the hint **Membalas akan membuka kembali tiket ini.** (Replying will reopen this ticket.) Your reply changes the ticket to **Ditugaskan** when its staff member is still there, or **Terbuka** when not.
3. Type in the **Balasan Anda** field. The limit is 2000 characters; the counter below the field shows the count.
4. To include a photo, tap the paperclip icon. Choose a source on the sheet "Lampirkan foto dari" (Attach a photo from): **Kamera** (Camera) or **Galeri** (Gallery), take or choose a photo, fill in **Keterangan (opsional)** (Caption (optional)) in the "Keterangan lampiran" (Attachment caption) window (at most 255 characters), then tap **Lampirkan** (Attach). The photo waits above the field with a **Lepas {nama}** (Remove {name}) button to take it back. You can include several photos.
5. Tap the send icon. The text is sent first, then the photos one by one. A part that fails stays in the box for the next tap, and text that was already sent is not sent twice.

Text and photos appear mixed in time order. Your replies on the right side are labelled "Anda" (You); replies from staff are labelled with the staff name or "Tim Inbils" (Inbils team). Each photo shows the sender, the time, and the caption. After you send, the screen scrolls to the newest message. After a **Diselesaikan** ticket is reopened, the app loads the new status and ISP staff receive a notification. When the ticket is closed while the screen is still open, the server rejects the reply and the card **Tiket ditutup. Masih bermasalah? Buat tiket baru** appears.

Photos can also be added when creating a ticket, through **Tambah lampiran** in the form. The app shrinks the photo first (JPEG, longest side 1920 pixels) and removes location data (EXIF) from the photos it sends. The server accepts jpg, jpeg, png, or pdf files with a size of at most 10 MB per file; from the app you only send photos.

- Photos appear in full in the timeline. Tap one to open it full screen; tap **Tutup** (Close) to go back. When the image does not load, it says "Gambar tidak bisa dimuat." (The image could not be loaded.) with a try-again button.
- PDFs from staff appear with their name ("Buka PDF {nama}" (Open PDF {name})). Tap one to download it; the app then opens the Android share sheet, where you choose a PDF reader app. When this fails, it says "PDF tidak bisa dibuka. Coba lagi." (The PDF could not be opened. Try again.)
- Other files appear with their name and size, but cannot be opened in the app.
- To delete a photo that you sent yourself, tap **Hapus lampiran** (Delete attachment), then **Hapus** (Delete) in the confirmation. Only the uploader can delete it, and only while the ticket is still Terbuka, Ditugaskan, or Dikerjakan. You cannot delete attachments from staff.
