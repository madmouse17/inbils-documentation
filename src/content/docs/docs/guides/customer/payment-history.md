---
title: "4. Payment history"
description: "See every payment recorded on your account, with its amount, method, date and invoice number, and where to find it in the app."
---

Open it from the **Akun** (Account) tab → **Riwayat pembayaran** (Payment history), or from the invoice details → **Lihat riwayat pembayaran** (View payment history).

![Payment history](~/assets/guide/customer/riwayat-pembayaran.webp)

Each row shows:

- the amount paid;
- the method;
- the date and time of the payment;
- the number of the invoice that was paid ("Tagihan INV-..." (Invoice INV-...)).

The possible methods are: **Pembayaran online** (Online payment) (through Midtrans), **Tunai** (Cash), **Transfer bank** (Bank transfer), **Cek** (Cheque), and **Lainnya** (Other). Cash, transfer, cheque, and other payments are recorded by ISP staff. Scroll down to load more; an empty list means "Belum ada pembayaran." (There are no payments yet.)

A payment that staff cancelled is corrected by the ISP; if there is a difference you do not understand, contact the ISP with the invoice number.
