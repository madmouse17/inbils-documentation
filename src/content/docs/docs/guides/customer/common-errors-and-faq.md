## Common errors


| Message or event | Cause and action |
| --- | --- |
| "Tidak ada koneksi internet. Periksa sinyal, lalu coba lagi." (No internet connection. Check your signal, then try again.) / banner "Anda sedang offline. Data yang tampil mungkin belum terbaru." (You are offline. The data shown may be out of date.) | The phone has no internet. The app does not keep a queue of actions: invoices cannot be paid, tickets are not sent, and replies are not sent until the connection is back. Connect the phone, then repeat. |
| "Sesi Anda berakhir. Silakan masuk lagi." (Your session has ended. Please sign in again.) | The login session has run out (90 days at most), the password was changed, or the device was signed out. Sign in again with your email and password. |
| "Email atau kata sandi salah." (Wrong email or password.) | This message appears below the **Email** field. Check the spelling of the email and the upper and lower case of the password. When you forgot it, use **Lupa kata sandi?** (Forgot password?). |
| "Demi keamanan, atur ulang kata sandi lewat link yang kami kirim ke email Anda." (For security, reset your password through the link we sent to your email.) | An old account whose password is still the phone number. Tap **Kirim link** (Send link) on the sheet that appears, open the email, and set a new password on the web page. Then sign in again in the app. |
| The password setup link does not work | The link is valid for 60 minutes and only one link is active per account; the newest link replaces the old one. Ask for a new link through **Lupa kata sandi?**. |
| "Akun ini tidak terdaftar sebagai pelanggan." (This account is not registered as a customer.) | The account is not yet linked to a customer record, is not active, or its email is not verified. Contact the ISP. |
| "Akun Anda dinonaktifkan. Hubungi kantor Inbils." (Your account has been deactivated. Contact the Inbils office.) | The ISP deactivated the account. Contact the ISP. |
| "Kode verifikasi kedaluwarsa. Silakan masuk lagi." (The verification code has expired. Please sign in again.) / "Kode salah." (Wrong code.) | The two-step code was late or wrong. Sign in again from the start; use a recovery code when needed. |
| "Terlalu banyak percobaan. Coba lagi dalam N detik." (Too many attempts. Try again in N seconds.) | Too many sign-in, code, password change, or payment attempts in a short time. Wait for the time shown. |
| "Permintaan sebelumnya masih diproses. Coba lagi sebentar lagi." (The previous request is still being processed. Try again in a moment.) | The previous tap is still running. Wait a few seconds; do not tap repeatedly. |
| "Data sudah berubah. Kami memuat ulang." (The data has changed. We are reloading.) | The data changed on the server while you were looking at it. Check the reloaded screen, then repeat. |
| "Server sedang bermasalah. Coba lagi beberapa saat lagi." (The server has a problem. Try again in a little while.) | A temporary fault on the server. Try again later. |
| "Halaman pembayaran tidak bisa dibuka. Coba lagi." (The payment page could not be opened. Try again.) / "Halaman pembayaran belum tersedia. Muat ulang tagihan, lalu coba lagi." (The payment page is not available yet. Reload the invoice, then try again.) | Repeat **Bayar** (Pay) after reloading the invoice. When it still fails, pay through the ISP. |
| "PDF tidak bisa dibuka. Coba lagi." (The PDF could not be opened. Try again.) / "Bukti bayar tidak bisa diunduh. Coba lagi." (The payment receipt could not be downloaded. Try again.) | The PDF download failed or there is no reader app. Check the internet, then tap again. |
| "Tiket ini sudah dinilai." (This ticket has already been rated.) / "Tiket ini belum diselesaikan, jadi belum bisa dinilai." (This ticket is not resolved, so it cannot be rated yet.) | The ticket changed while you were rating it. The app reloads the ticket; check its status. |
| "Pembayaran online belum tersedia. Silakan bayar lewat kantor Inbils." (Online payment is not available yet. Please pay at the Inbils office.) | Online payment is not active at the moment. Contact the ISP. |
| Paid but the invoice is still Belum dibayar (Unpaid) | Tap **Periksa status** (Check status). The system also checks Midtrans regularly. When it does not change, contact the ISP with the invoice number and the proof of payment. |
| An attachment fails to upload | File limits: jpg, jpeg, png, or pdf, at most 10 MB. Try taking the photo again or choose it from the gallery. You cannot add attachments to a ticket that is already Ditutup (Closed). |
| "Foto tidak dapat diambil. Coba lagi." (The photo could not be taken. Try again.) | The app cannot get a photo from the camera or the gallery. Check the camera or storage permission of the app in Android settings. |
| "Data tidak ditemukan atau sudah dihapus." (The data was not found or has been deleted.) | The ticket or invoice no longer exists. Go back to the list. |


## FAQ


**Who creates my account?** Your ISP. You do not register yourself. The first time you sign in, set your password through the link in the email.

**Can I use the website to pay invoices?** No. Invoices and payments go through the app. The website is only used to reset a password through the email link and to manage two-step verification.

**How do I turn on two-step verification?** On the website, not in the app. Sign in on the website, tap **Kelola akun & 2FA** (Manage account & 2FA), and follow the instructions. Once it is on, the app asks for a code when you sign in.

**I forgot my password.** Tap **Lupa kata sandi?** on the sign-in screen and follow the email that is sent.

**Can I change my login email?** Not in the app. Contact the ISP.

**How do I change the address, invoice name, or package?** Contact the ISP. The profile and subscriptions in the app are read-only; the only things you can change yourself are the account name, phone, and password.

**My service is isolir (internet blocked while the account is suspended). What should I do?** Pay the open invoices through the app (use mobile data). Once they are paid, the service becomes active again automatically. See [chapter 5](/docs/guides/customer/subscriptions/).

**My ticket is already Diselesaikan (Resolved). How do I close it?** In the ticket details, tap **Sudah beres** (Solved) on the card "Masalah sudah beres?" (Is the problem solved?) and give a rating. When it is not solved, tap **Masih bermasalah** (Still a problem) and reply to the ticket; the ticket is reopened.

**Why does "Anda sudah punya tiket aktif" (You already have an active ticket) appear?** You are reporting a problem with the same category and service as a ticket that is not finished. Tap **Lihat tiket** (View ticket) to reply there, or **Tetap buat baru** (Create a new one anyway) when the problem is different.

**My phone is lost.** From another phone, sign in, open **Akun** (Account) → **Perangkat** (Devices) and **Keluarkan** (Sign out) the lost phone, then change your password. When you cannot sign in, contact the ISP.

**How long do I stay signed in?** Until you sign out, change your password, or sign out a device; 90 days at most, and the app renews the session by itself when it is used.

**Why do I not receive WhatsApp messages?** Perhaps the ISP has not enabled WhatsApp, has not recorded your consent, your number is not a valid Indonesian mobile number, or you once replied STOP. Notifications in the app are still available through the bell icon. Contact the ISP to change this.

**App notifications do not appear on the phone screen.** Open **Akun** → **Notifikasi uji** (Test notification), tap **Kirim notifikasi uji** (Send test notification), and follow the **Perbaiki** (Fix) advice on the check that fails ([chapter 7](/docs/guides/customer/notifications/)). Contact the ISP when the permission and token are correct but the test message still does not arrive.

**Do my photos contain my location?** No. The app removes the location data (EXIF) and shrinks the photo before uploading it.
