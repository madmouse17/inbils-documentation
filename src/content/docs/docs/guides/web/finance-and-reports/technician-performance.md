---
title: "7.9 Technician performance (Kinerja Teknisi)"
description: "See one technician's completed work orders, resolved tickets, SLA compliance, evaluation scores, first response time and workload, or rank all technicians."
---

Kinerja Teknisi (Technician performance) shows the results of one technician: completed work orders (SPK), resolved tickets, SLA compliance, average evaluation score, average first response and active workload. With no technician selected, the screen shows a ranking of all technicians in one table. Select one technician to read the details.

Roles that can use it on the web: an admin with `report.manage`, or a manager with `report.technician`, an active employee profile and a placement in an active organisation. An admin can select all active technicians in the company. A manager can select only technicians in their own unit and the units below it. The server rules also allow a technician role with `report.technician` to see themselves, but technician accounts are directed to the Android app.

## Selecting a technician and reading the results

1. In the sidebar, open **Keuangan & Laporan** (Finance and Reports), then click **Kinerja Teknisi**. You can also open **Laporan** (Reports) and click the link **Kinerja Teknisi**.

   ![Technician performance page with the Technician selector, the Period field, the Show button, and the Technician ranking table](~/assets/guide/web/reports-technician.webp)

2. In **Teknisi** (Technician) (optional, hint **Semua teknisi** (All technicians)), type at least 2 letters of the technician's name or email, wait for the list to appear, then select one. This box only searches, so there is no list before you type. The list contains at most 20 results and holds only active users with the technician role and an active employee profile in the company. The selection stays until you select another technician or click the button **Hapus pilihan: Teknisi** (Clear selection: Technician).
3. Fill in **Periode** (Period) if needed. Type a range in the company date format, or click **Buka kalender** (Open calendar), select the start date and then the end date, or use a quick choice (**Hari ini** (Today), **7 hari terakhir** (Last 7 days), **30 hari terakhir** (Last 30 days), **Bulan ini** (This month), **Bulan lalu** (Last month), **Tahun ini** (This year)). Click **Hapus rentang tanggal** (Clear date range) to empty it. An empty field (**Semua tanggal** (All dates)) means no limit.
4. Click **Tampilkan** (Show). The button is also active without a technician: in that case only the period is used and the table **Peringkat teknisi** (Technician ranking) answers it.
5. With no technician selected, read the table **Peringkat teknisi**: "Menurut jumlah SPK dan tiket yang selesai pada periode. Pilih satu teknisi untuk melihat rinciannya." (By the number of work orders and tickets completed in the period. Select one technician to see the details.) The table contains all technicians within your account's scope, with the columns **Peringkat** (Rank), **Teknisi** (link), **SPK selesai** (Completed SPK), **Tiket diselesaikan** (Resolved tickets), **Kepatuhan SLA** (SLA compliance), **Respons pertama** (First response), **Skor evaluasi** (Evaluation score) and **Beban aktif** (Active load). The order follows the number of completed SPK plus resolved tickets, with the highest at the top. Technicians with the same count share a rank and are sorted by name. Click a technician's name to open the details with the same period. A technician without resolved tickets has no **Kepatuhan SLA**, and it is written **-**. **Respons pertama** is written for example `2 jam 15 menit` (2 hours 15 minutes). If your account has no technician in scope, the screen writes "Tidak ada teknisi yang bisa dipilih." (There is no technician to select.)
6. With a technician selected, read the six cards. Each figure is written with its unit (`SPK`, `tiket`, `dari 5`, `pekerjaan`) (SPK, tickets, out of 5, jobs) and percentages use a decimal comma, for example `63,6%`:
   - **SPK selesai** counts SPK with the status Selesai (Completed) that were assigned to the technician and completed within the **Periode**.
   - **Tiket diselesaikan** counts tickets with the status Diselesaikan (Resolved) or Ditutup (Closed) that were assigned to the technician and have a completion time within the **Periode**.
   - **Kepatuhan SLA** is the percentage of Tiket diselesaikan that were completed no later than the SLA limit. Tickets without an SLA limit are still counted, but are never treated as within SLA.
   - **Rata-rata skor evaluasi** (Average evaluation score) is the average score in Evaluasi (Evaluations) ([section 3.5](/docs/guides/web/operations/evaluations/)) whose evaluation date falls within the **Periode**.
   - **Rata-rata respons pertama** (Average first response) is the average time from ticket creation to first response, for Tiket diselesaikan that have a first response. It is written in hours and minutes, for example `45 menit` (45 minutes) or `2 jam 15 menit`.
   - **Beban kerja aktif** (Active workload) counts SPK with the status Ditugaskan (Assigned) or Dikerjakan (In progress), plus tickets with the status Terbuka (Open), Ditugaskan or Dikerjakan that are currently assigned to the technician.
7. Click **Lihat data** (View data) under a card to open the source list ([section 7.3](/docs/guides/web/finance-and-reports/reports/)). The link appears only if your account may open the source module. The **Beban kerja aktif** card has two links: **Lihat SPK** (View SPK) for SPK and **Lihat tiket** (View tickets) for tickets.
8. Read the three charts:
   - **SPK selesai per hari** (Completed SPK per day), **SPK selesai per minggu** (per week) or **SPK selesai per bulan** (per month) shows completed SPK across the **Periode**. The unit follows the length of the period. The unit still in progress today is marked "(berjalan)" (in progress).
   - **SPK selesai menurut jenis** (Completed SPK by type) separates Instalasi (Installation), Perawatan (Maintenance), Peningkatan layanan (Service upgrade) and Pindah lokasi (Relocation).
   - **Tiket selesai menurut kategori** (Completed tickets by category) divides Tiket diselesaikan by ticket category. The bar chart shows at most 10 rows, and the rest are merged into **Lainnya** (Other).
9. Click **Ekspor** (Export) after the results appear, then select **Ekspor CSV** (Export CSV) or **Ekspor PDF** (Export PDF). This menu exists only if your account holds `report.export`. The file columns are Metrik, Rincian, Nilai, Satuan, Cakupan, Filter and Versi, with the technician's name in Filter. The file also contains Rata-rata waktu SPK, Rata-rata waktu tiket, Rata-rata rating pelanggan, SPK per jenis and Tiket per kategori, which do not show as cards. The ranking is not exported.

## Common errors

- The menu is missing or the page shows **Akses ditolak** (Access denied): the manager does not hold `report.technician`, has no active profile, or has not been placed in an active organisation. Correct the data in Karyawan (Employees) ([section 8.3](/docs/guides/web/administration/employees/)) and Organisasi (Organisation) ([section 8.2](/docs/guides/web/administration/organisation/)).
- A technician does not appear in the list: the account is inactive, the employee profile is not active, the role is not technician, the company is different, or the position is outside the manager's unit. Type at least 2 letters, because the **Teknisi** box shows "Ketik minimal 2 huruf untuk mencari." (Type at least 2 letters to search.) before that, and "Tidak ada hasil." (No results.) when nothing matches.
- The message **Tidak ada teknisi yang bisa dipilih.** appears when your account has no technician in scope, or the last search found no technician. With no technician selected, the page shows the ranking, not the message **Pilih teknisi untuk melihat laporan.** (Select a technician to see the report.), which is no longer used.
- A value shown as a hyphen (-) means there is no data to average in that period. A figure of 0 means the matching count is really zero.
- **Beban kerja aktif** is the current position. This figure does not change when **Periode** changes.
- **Kepatuhan SLA** here uses only tickets with the status Diselesaikan or Ditutup that belong to the technician. The figure can differ from Kepatuhan SLA in [section 7.6](/docs/guides/web/finance-and-reports/sla-compliance/).
- **Tanggal akhir tidak boleh sebelum tanggal mulai.** (The end date must not be before the start date.) appears under **Periode** when the range is reversed. Input that is not a date produces **Format tanggal tidak valid.** (Invalid date format.) with an example of the format.
