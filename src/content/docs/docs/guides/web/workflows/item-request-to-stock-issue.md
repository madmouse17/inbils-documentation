---
title: "9.3 Item request to stock issue"
description: "Follow items from request to approval, reservation, preparation, handover and the return of leftovers, with the separation of duties at each point."
---

The third workflow follows items from the request until they leave the warehouse, and then the leftovers come back. It involves the requester (usually a technician), the approver (manager), the warehouse officer (staff), and the person who receives the return. Separation of duties applies at every point, including for admins.

## Steps

1. The requester creates the request. A technician creates it in the technician app, and the request gets status **Menunggu persetujuan** (Awaiting approval) at once. On the web, only a role that holds `inventory.request.create` (admin in the default roles) sees **Buat Permintaan Barang** (Create item request) in **Permintaan Barang** (Item requests) ([section 6.1](/docs/guides/web/inventory/item-requests/)). Fill in **Gudang Tujuan** (Destination warehouse), **Alasan Kebutuhan Barang** (Reason for item need) (required), and **Tanggal Dibutuhkan** (Date needed) if needed. In **Rujukan (opsional)** (Reference (optional)), select a work order (SPK) or ticket, then type its code to search and select. In **Barang 1** (Item 1), type the name or SKU of a consumable item and fill in **Jumlah** (Quantity). Click **Tambah Barang** (Add item) for the next row. Click **Ajukan ke Antrean Gudang** (Submit to warehouse queue). **Simpan sebagai Draf** (Save as draft) saves without submitting, and a draft is submitted later with **Ajukan ke Gudang** (Submit to warehouse) on the detail page. Make sure **Gudang Tujuan** is filled in: the approval dialog on the web has no warehouse choice, so a request without a warehouse cannot be approved from the web. Each request gets a number in the form `REQ-<tahun>-<5 angka>`, for example REQ-2026-00001, and the count starts again from 00001 each year. Companies that still use the old format (`REQ-20261020-0003`, daily count) are moved to this format, except those that already changed their format in Numbering ([section 7.13](/docs/guides/web/finance-and-reports/numbering/)). Old numbers stay valid. **Rujukan** appears in the list and on the detail page as the code that people use, for example "SPK SPK-2026-00012", not as a random code.
2. A holder of the approve permission receives the notification **Permintaan Barang Baru** (New item request) in the bell. The request goes into **Persetujuan** (Approvals) and the card **Menunggu persetujuan saya** (Awaiting my approval) on the Dashboard.
3. A manager or admin who is not the requester decides. A user who may not decide sees the text "Menunggu persetujuan Manajer/Admin" (Awaiting Manager/Admin approval) on the detail page and in the **Aksi Berikutnya** (Next action) column of the list, not a button. A rejection gives the requester a bell and push notification with the reason.
4. When approved, Inbils checks the available stock in the **Gudang Tujuan**. If it is enough, the stock is reserved, the status becomes **Disetujui** (Approved), and the requester receives a bell and push notification. If it is not enough, nothing is reserved and the message names the item, the warehouse, the available quantity, and the required quantity.
5. A warehouse officer who is not the approver opens the tab **Persiapan & Antrean Gudang** (Preparation & Warehouse Queue), then clicks **Siapkan barang** (Prepare items). This one click without a dialog prepares the items and marks them ready: the status becomes **Siap diambil** (Ready for pickup) at once, the toast "Barang disiapkan dan siap diambil." (Items prepared and ready for pickup.) appears, and the requester receives a bell and push notification. The history still records both steps, **Disiapkan** (Prepared) and then **Siap diambil**. While the items have not been handed over, the item list shows the column **Ambil dari** (Pick from) (the location of each item). A request that has been left in status **Disiapkan** since before this update is finished with the same button.
6. When the requester receives the physical items, an officer who is neither the requester nor the approver clicks **Serah terima** (Handover), then **Konfirmasi serah terima** (Confirm handover) in the dialog "Konfirmasi serah terima barang?" (Confirm item handover?). The status becomes **Diserahkan** (Handed over). The reservation is released (**Mutasi Stok** (Stock movements) records **Cadangan dilepas** (Reservation released)), physical stock decreases (**Keluar** (Out)), and **Jumlah Diserahkan** (Quantity handed over) is filled in. A handover cannot be undone.
7. Inbils opens a return file for that request with status **Menunggu deklarasi teknisi** (Awaiting technician declaration). After the work is done, the technician reports the actual usage and the leftover items in the technician app. The file owner who works on the web uses **Laporkan Pemakaian & Ajukan Pengembalian** (Report Usage & Submit Return) on the request detail page, fills in **Dipakai Aktual** (Actually used) and **Sisa Dikembalikan** (Leftover returned) for each item, then clicks **Kirim Laporan Deklarasi** (Submit Declaration Report). The quantity used, the good leftover, and the difference must add up to the **Diserahkan** quantity. The file becomes **Menunggu penerimaan gudang** (Awaiting warehouse receipt), and stock does not change yet.
8. A holder of `inventory.return.receive` who is not the file owner opens **Permintaan Barang**, tab **Pengembalian material** (Material returns) ([section 6.8](/docs/guides/web/inventory/material-returns/)), then clicks **Buka Permintaan & Terima Barang** (Open Request & Receive Items). On the detail page, click **Terima Pengembalian Fisik** (Receive Physical Return), fill in **Baik** (Good), **Rusak** (Damaged), **Hilang** (Lost) and **Lainnya** (Other) for each item, then click **Konfirmasi Penerimaan Fisik** (Confirm Physical Receipt). Under each item, a line "Jumlah cocok" (Quantity matches), "Kurang &lt;n&gt;" (Short by &lt;n&gt;) or "Lebih &lt;n&gt;" (Over by &lt;n&gt;) shows whether the numbers already equal what was handed over, and the button can be used only when everything matches. Good items go back into stock, and **Mutasi Stok** records them as **Pengembalian** (Return), not **Masuk** (In), so that they can be told apart from purchase receipts.
9. If there are no damaged, lost or other items, the file becomes **Diterima** (Received). If there are, the file becomes **Selisih perlu penyelesaian** (Difference needs resolution), and holders of `inventory.return.resolve` receive a notification in the bell. A manager who is not the physical receiver resolves the difference with a note.
10. If the items did not arrive or do not match the report, reject the physical handover. The file becomes **Perlu koreksi** (Needs correction). The technician receives a bell and push notification with the reason and reports again.
11. Check the result. **Stok** (Stock) ([section 6.5](/docs/guides/web/inventory/stock/)) shows **Jumlah** (Quantity), **Dicadangkan** (Reserved), and **Tersedia** (Available). **Mutasi Stok** ([section 6.7](/docs/guides/web/inventory/stock-movements/)) shows the trail of **Dicadangkan**, **Cadangan dilepas**, **Keluar**, and **Pengembalian** for that request. The **Keluar** movement record contains the request code, so the code can be searched in the box **Cari mutasi** (Search movements).

A request that is still **Draf** (Draft) or **Menunggu persetujuan** can be cancelled with **Batalkan Permintaan** (Cancel Request). The requester cancels their own request if their role holds `inventory.request.cancel.own` (admin in the default roles). A holder of `inventory.request.approve` who does not hold that permission (manager in the default roles) can cancel anyone's request. After approval, this screen offers no cancellation. The timeline on the detail page stops at **Ditolak** (Rejected) or **Dibatalkan** (Cancelled) for a request that ends there, with who and when, without showing steps that were never reached.

## Relationship with work orders (SPK)

If the request uses a work order **Rujukan**, its return file is linked to that work order.

- A technician cannot submit the work order for review while there is a file that has not been reported, that is **Menunggu deklarasi teknisi** or **Perlu koreksi**. The message reads "Seluruh sisa material dari permintaan barang harus dideklarasikan terlebih dahulu sebelum SPK diajukan." (All leftover material from the item request must be declared first before the work order is submitted.)
- The approver cannot approve the work order until all its files are **Diterima** or **Selisih diselesaikan** (Difference resolved). The message reads "SPK belum bisa disetujui sampai seluruh retur material berstatus direkonsiliasi atau diselesaikan." (The work order cannot be approved until all material returns are reconciled or resolved.)
- When the work order is approved, the quantity used on the work order materials is adjusted to **Dipakai Aktual**, and the stock of materials decreases only for the part that has not yet left through an item request.

## Common errors

- The approval dialog shows "Stok &lt;barang&gt; di &lt;gudang&gt; tidak cukup: tersedia &lt;n&gt; &lt;satuan&gt;, diminta &lt;m&gt; &lt;satuan&gt;." (Stock of &lt;item&gt; in &lt;warehouse&gt; is not enough: &lt;n&gt; &lt;unit&gt; available, &lt;m&gt; &lt;unit&gt; requested.) (for example "Stok Kabel Drop di Gudang Pusat tidak cukup: tersedia 3 m, diminta 5 m." (Stock of Drop Cable in Central Warehouse is not enough: 3 m available, 5 m requested.)): reduce the approved quantity, or add stock with **Terima Barang** (Receive items) in **Stok** ([section 6.5](/docs/guides/web/inventory/stock/)), then approve again. If it cannot be fulfilled, reject the request with a reason.
- The approval dialog shows "Lokasi gudang sumber wajib ditentukan untuk reservasi." (The source warehouse location must be specified for reservation.): the request was saved without a **Gudang Tujuan**, and the approval dialog on the web has no warehouse choice. Such a request cannot be approved from the web ([section 6.1](/docs/guides/web/inventory/item-requests/)). Cancel it with **Batalkan Permintaan**, then create a new request with **Gudang Tujuan** filled in.
- The button **Setujui & Reservasi Stok** (Approve & Reserve Stock), **Siapkan barang**, or **Serah terima** does not appear: check the request status and your permissions, and whether you are the requester or the approver of that request. A requester must not approve or hand over their own request, and an approver must not prepare or hand over items that they approved.
- A role that does not contain `roles.manage` must not hold the approve permission together with the prepare or hand over permission. In the default templates, the Manager approves while Warehouse and Staff prepare and hand over. If your company has only one warehouse person who is also an approver, add a second person. When such a role is saved, Inbils refuses with a message that begins "Pemisahan tugas (persetujuan permintaan barang vs pelaksanaan gudang)" (Separation of duties (item request approval vs warehouse execution)).
- The tab **Pengembalian material** shows an error box: your role holds the return permissions but not `inventory.return.view.all` or `inventory.return.view.own`. Ask the company admin to add it to the role in the Roles menu ([section 8.6](/docs/guides/web/administration/roles/)).
- A message that begins "Jumlah tidak cocok untuk" (Quantity does not match for) followed by an item name appears when the numbers for one item do not add up to the handed over quantity. Change the numbers until they match.
- A stock quantity correction that is not the result of an item request uses **Ajukan Penyesuaian** (Request Adjustment) in **Stok**, which needs approval by another person ([section 6.5](/docs/guides/web/inventory/stock/)).
