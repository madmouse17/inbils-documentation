---
title: "9.2 Ticket to work order"
description: "Follow one service fault from the first report to a closed ticket, including the repair work order, approval and reopening."
---

The second workflow follows one service fault from the report until the ticket is closed, through one repair work order (SPK). It involves the staff member or admin who logs the ticket, the admin or manager who assigns it, and the technician who does the work.

A fault that is fixed remotely goes through steps 5 to 9 without a work order: the handler writes the completion note directly in step 10. A repair work order is needed only if a site visit is required.

## Steps

1. Log the ticket. Open **Tiket** (Tickets) ([section 3.4](/docs/guides/web/operations/tickets/)), click **Buat tiket** (Create ticket), fill in **Judul** (Title), **Sumber** (Source) (**Pelanggan** (Customer), **NOC**, or **Internal**), **Kategori** (Category), **Prioritas** (Priority), and **Pelanggan** for a customer ticket, then click **Simpan** (Save). Fill in **Langganan** (Subscription), **Aset jaringan** (Network asset), and **Lokasi** (Location) if known. The fields **Pelanggan**, **Langganan**, and **Aset jaringan** are search boxes: type at least 2 characters, then select from at most 20 results. If the selected customer has only one subscription, **Langganan** fills itself in. **Judul** is filled with the name of the **Kategori** until you write your own title. As soon as a **Kategori** is selected, one line below the form reads "Target respons &lt;waktu&gt;, selesai &lt;waktu&gt;" (Response target &lt;time&gt;, resolution &lt;time&gt;) (or "Tanpa target respons, selesai &lt;waktu&gt;" (No response target, resolution &lt;time&gt;)), calculated from the category, subscription and priority currently selected, so you know the ticket targets before saving. Customers can also create a ticket themselves from the customer app, and it appears in the same list. The customer receives a WhatsApp message that the ticket is logged if the company WhatsApp is active and the customer has agreed. The same conditions apply to every WhatsApp message to the customer in this workflow.
2. A new ticket has status **Terbuka** (Open). **Target respons** (Response target) and **Target penyelesaian** (Resolution target) are calculated from the creation time. The source is the SLA tier version of the ticket's subscription package (it still applies even if the tier has since been deactivated), or the **Kategori Tiket** (Ticket categories) if the ticket has no subscription, and the ticket page names the source next to both targets. Priority **Mendesak** (Urgent) cuts both in half, rounded up. If the **Hitungan tenggat** (Deadline clock) of the SLA tier is **Jam layanan** (Service hours), only the company opening hours are counted ([section 8.1](/docs/guides/web/administration/company/)): a ticket that arrives outside opening hours starts counting at the next opening, and the ticket page reads "Dihitung sejak tiket dibuat, dalam jam layanan:" (Counted from ticket creation, within service hours:). An SLA tier of **24 jam** (24 hours) and the Ticket categories count day and night. A ticket that passes its **Target penyelesaian** appears in the card **SLA terlampaui** (SLA exceeded) on the Dashboard ([section 2.1](/docs/guides/web/dashboard/queue-cards/)).
3. Assign a handler. An admin or manager opens **Penugasan** (Dispatch) ([section 3.1](/docs/guides/web/operations/dispatch/)), finds the ticket, clicks **Tugaskan** (Assign), selects a handler in **Pilih petugas tiket** (Select ticket handler), then clicks **Simpan penugasan** (Save assignment). From a ticket page with status **Terbuka**, the **Tugaskan** button opens a dialog with the choice **Petugas** (Handler) (type to search, then select). The options in both places read "&lt;nama&gt; · &lt;n&gt; pekerjaan aktif" (&lt;name&gt; · &lt;n&gt; active jobs) (the tickets and work orders the person currently holds), and the handler with the fewest jobs is labelled "Paling sedikit pekerjaan" (Fewest jobs) when the workloads are not equal. A holder of `ticket.assign` who may also handle tickets sees the button **Tugaskan ke saya** (Assign to me) in the card **Tindakan** (Actions) on an **Terbuka** ticket that has no handler yet, unless the company forbids self-assignment. The toast reads "&lt;kode tiket&gt; ditugaskan ke &lt;nama&gt;." (&lt;ticket code&gt; assigned to &lt;name&gt;.) from **Penugasan**, "Tiket berhasil ditugaskan." (Ticket assigned successfully.) from the dialog on the ticket page, and "Tiket &lt;kode tiket&gt; ditugaskan ke Anda." (Ticket &lt;ticket code&gt; assigned to you.) from **Tugaskan ke saya**. The status becomes **Ditugaskan** (Assigned). The handler receives an email and a bell notification. On the first assignment, the customer receives a WhatsApp message that the ticket is being handled.
4. The handler starts the ticket. A technician does this in the technician app. A web user who holds `ticket.start` uses **Mulai** (Start) on the ticket page or in **Pekerjaan Saya** (My Work) ([section 3.6](/docs/guides/web/operations/my-work/)). The status becomes **Dikerjakan** (In progress) and **Respons pertama** (First response) is filled in.
5. If the fault needs a visit, an admin or manager with the `ticket.spawn_spk` permission opens a ticket with status **Ditugaskan** or **Dikerjakan** that has no completion note yet, clicks **Buat SPK** (Create work order) in the card **Tindakan**, then confirms with **Buat SPK** in the dialog. Inbils creates a **Perawatan** (Maintenance) work order with status **Menunggu penugasan** (Awaiting assignment) and the title "SPK dari tiket &lt;kode tiket&gt;" (Work order from ticket &lt;ticket code&gt;), then opens that work order page at once with the toast "SPK berhasil dibuat dari tiket." (Work order created from ticket successfully.) **Pelanggan**, **Langganan**, **Lokasi**, **Prioritas**, and the description are copied from the ticket. The ticket's network asset is not copied to the work order.
6. The ticket does not complete with it and does not show a link to that work order. To find the work order again, search for the ticket code in the **Cari** (Search) box in **SPK** (Work orders) ([section 3.3](/docs/guides/web/operations/work-orders/)), because the work order title contains the ticket code. One ticket can have only one work order created from it.
7. Assign the work order. Open **Penugasan** or the work order page, click **Tugaskan**, then select a technician, fill in **Tanggal kunjungan** (Visit date) if needed, and save. The work order status becomes **Ditugaskan** and the technician receives a WhatsApp message. A work order from a ticket is created without a schedule date. The customer receives a schedule message on the first assignment only if you fill in **Tanggal kunjungan**.
8. The technician works on the work order in the app: starts it, records materials, uploads evidence photos, then submits the result. Materials taken from the warehouse follow workflow 9.3.
9. A manager or admin who is neither the creator of the work order nor its technician approves the work order in **Persetujuan** (Approvals) ([section 3.2](/docs/guides/web/operations/approvals/)). The creator of the work order is the person who pressed **Buat SPK** on the ticket, so that person needs a colleague to approve. The work order becomes **Selesai** (Completed), and the stock of the materials used decreases at the work order **Lokasi** if the work order has a location.
10. The ticket handler fills in the completion note: click **Selesaikan** (Complete), fill in **Catatan penyelesaian** (Completion note) (required, maximum 1000 characters), then click **Selesaikan** in the dialog. The status becomes **Diselesaikan** (Resolved) and the customer receives a WhatsApp message that the ticket is complete. A ticket can be completed only from status **Dikerjakan**. In the customer app, a ticket that is **Diselesaikan** shows the card "Masalah sudah beres?" (Is the problem fixed?). A customer who taps **Sudah beres** (Fixed) gives a rating from 1 to 5 (the comment is optional), and the ticket becomes **Ditutup** (Closed) at once without step 11. A customer who taps **Masih bermasalah** (Still a problem) replies to the ticket, and that reply reopens the ticket.
11. If the customer has not answered, a holder of `ticket.close` who is not the handler of that ticket opens the ticket, clicks **Tutup** (Close), then **Tutup tiket** (Close ticket) in the dialog titled "Tutup tiket &lt;kode tiket&gt;?" (Close ticket &lt;ticket code&gt;?) ("Pelanggan tidak bisa membalas lagi; tiket masuk laporan." (The customer can no longer reply; the ticket enters the reports.)). The status becomes **Ditutup**. The customer can still rate a ticket that is **Ditutup** and not yet rated, with the button **Beri nilai** (Rate) in the app. The ticket page shows the rating in **Penilaian pelanggan** (Customer rating), for example "4 dari 5" (4 out of 5).
12. If the problem turns out not to be fixed, a holder of `ticket.reopen` (Admin, Manajer (Manager) and NOC & Layanan Pelanggan (NOC & Customer Service) in the default templates, see 10.1) opens a ticket that is **Diselesaikan** or **Ditutup**, clicks **Buka kembali** (Reopen), fills in **Alasan** (Reason) (required, 5 to 500 characters), then clicks **Buka kembali** in the dialog "Buka kembali tiket &lt;kode tiket&gt;?" (Reopen ticket &lt;ticket code&gt;?). The toast "Tiket &lt;kode tiket&gt; dibuka kembali." (Ticket &lt;ticket code&gt; reopened.) appears. The ticket returns to **Ditugaskan** if it still has a handler, or **Terbuka** if not, with the badge "Dibuka kembali &lt;tanggal&gt;" (Reopened &lt;date&gt;) in the card **Status**. The completion note and the completed or closed time are cleared (the old note remains in the **Log Audit** (Audit log)). The target times are not recalculated, and the customer's rating stays saved. The ticket handler (or a holder of `ticket.assign` if there is no handler yet) receives the notification "Tiket &lt;kode tiket&gt; dibuka kembali" (Ticket &lt;ticket code&gt; reopened) containing the reason, and the customer is told that the ticket will be handled again.
13. After that, create an evaluation (**Evaluasi** (Evaluations), [section 3.5](/docs/guides/web/operations/evaluations/)) for that ticket or work order with **Buat evaluasi** (Create evaluation) if needed. If you open it from the button **Evaluasi teknisi** (Evaluate technician) on a ticket page that the customer has rated, the field **Penilaian pelanggan (opsional)** (Customer rating (optional)) is filled with that rating, while the evaluator score stays empty. The ticket response and resolution times feed into **Kepatuhan SLA** (SLA compliance) and **Kinerja Teknisi** (Technician performance) ([section 7.6](/docs/guides/web/finance-and-reports/sla-compliance/) and [7.9](/docs/guides/web/finance-and-reports/technician-performance/)).

## Common errors

- **Buat SPK** does not appear: the ticket must be **Ditugaskan** or **Dikerjakan** and have no completion note yet. If the button appears but the server refuses, the cause is one of these: you do not hold `ticket.spawn_spk`, or the ticket already has a work order ("SPK sudah dibuat dari tiket ini." (A work order has already been created from this ticket.)).
- You cannot approve the repair work order yourself because you are its creator. The server refuses with the message "Pembuat SPK tidak boleh menyetujui SPK sendiri." (The creator of a work order must not approve it.) Ask a colleague who is allowed to.
- Approving a **Perawatan** work order does not create an installation invoice. The installation invoice workflow applies only to **Instalasi** (Installation) work orders.
- **Selesaikan** is refused on a **Ditugaskan** ticket: the handler must click **Mulai** first. The message reads "Tiket harus berstatus dikerjakan untuk bisa diselesaikan." (The ticket must be in progress to be completed.)
- **Tutup** does not appear: the ticket is not yet **Diselesaikan**, you do not hold `ticket.close`, or you are the handler of that ticket. A customer who taps **Sudah beres** in the app closes the ticket themselves without that rule.
- **Buka kembali** does not appear: the ticket is not yet **Diselesaikan** or **Ditutup**, or your role does not hold `ticket.reopen` (see [section 8.6](/docs/guides/web/administration/roles/) and [10.1](/docs/guides/web/appendix/roles-and-permissions-summary/)). The dialog refuses an empty reason or one shorter than 5 characters. If the ticket has already changed in the meantime, the message reads "Hanya tiket yang diselesaikan atau ditutup yang bisa dibuka kembali." (Only resolved or closed tickets can be reopened.)
- A **Diselesaikan** ticket is reopened when the customer replies. A **Ditutup** ticket does not accept replies from the customer; staff can still reopen it with **Buka kembali**, or the customer creates a new ticket.
