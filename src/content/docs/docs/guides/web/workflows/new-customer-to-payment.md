---
title: "9.1 New customer to payment"
description: "Follow one customer from registration through installation to a paid first invoice, with the role, menu and screen for each step."
---

This chapter links the menus in [chapter 3](/docs/guides/web/operations/dispatch/) to 8 into three workflows. Each step names who acts, the menu to use, and the section that explains the screen.

The first workflow follows one customer from registration until the first invoice is paid. It involves an admin or manager, a technician, and the customer.

Prepare first:

- An active service package with an active bandwidth profile, speed profile and SLA tier ([section 4.2](/docs/guides/web/customers-and-services/service-packages/) to 4.5).
- The POP location that serves the customer's address ([section 5.1](/docs/guides/web/network/locations/)). If you want to record the ODP drop port from the start, also prepare an ODP with a free port ([section 5.5](/docs/guides/web/network/odn/)).
- An active technician account with an active employee profile ([section 8.3](/docs/guides/web/administration/employees/)) and the technician app installed.
- Two different people to register and to approve the work order (SPK). The person who creates the work order must not approve it ([section 3.3](/docs/guides/web/operations/work-orders/)).
- An ONT device as an asset with status **Tersedia** (Available) in **Aset Jaringan** (Network assets) ([section 5.2](/docs/guides/web/network/network-assets/)), if the work order will install a device.

## Steps

1. An admin or manager opens **Pelanggan** (Customers) ([section 4.1](/docs/guides/web/customers-and-services/customers/)) and clicks **Buat Pelanggan** (Create customer). The default staff role does not see this button because registration also needs the subscription permission (`customer.subscription.manage`).
2. Fill in all four tabs: **Data pelanggan** (Customer data), **Alamat** (Address), **Kontak** (Contacts), and **Langganan & SPK** (Subscription & work order). In **Data pelanggan**, tick **Pelanggan setuju menerima notifikasi WhatsApp** (Customer agrees to receive WhatsApp notifications) only if the customer has agreed, and select **Diperoleh oleh** (Acquired by) if the sale must be credited (type to search, then select; [section 7.10](/docs/guides/web/finance-and-reports/sales-kpis/)). In **Langganan & SPK**, select **Paket layanan** (Service package) and **POP pelayan** (Serving POP) in the same way. Holders of `spk.assign` also see the **Tugaskan sekarang** (Assign now) block with the fields **Teknisi** (Technician) (type to search; the list shows each technician's workload) and **Tanggal kunjungan** (Visit date). Fill them in if the installation is already agreed, or leave them empty to assign later from **Penugasan** (Dispatch) (step 5). Without `spk.assign`, the block is named **Jadwal kunjungan** (Visit schedule) and contains only **Tanggal kunjungan**. The visit date must not be before today ("Tanggal kunjungan tidak boleh sebelum hari ini." (The visit date cannot be before today.)). Click **Buat pelanggan dan SPK** (Create customer and work order).
3. In one transaction, Inbils creates the customer, a sign-in account with the `customer` role, the address, the contact, a subscription with status **Menunggu** (Pending), and an **Instalasi** (Installation) work order. The work order has status **Menunggu penugasan** (Awaiting assignment), or **Ditugaskan** (Assigned) if you chose a **Teknisi** in **Tugaskan sekarang** (a failed assignment cancels the whole registration). The work order is titled "Pemasangan &lt;kode langganan&gt; - &lt;nama pelanggan&gt;" (Installation &lt;subscription code&gt; - &lt;customer name&gt;), uses the **POP pelayan** as its **Lokasi** (Location), and carries the **Tanggal kunjungan** if you filled it in. The customer status is still **Prospek** (Prospect). Inbils then opens the customer details and shows the toast "Pelanggan, langganan, dan SPK pemasangan dibuat. Tautan akses akan dikirim ke &lt;email&gt;." (Customer, subscription and installation work order created. An access link will be sent to &lt;email&gt;.) If you chose a technician, the first sentence reads "Pelanggan, langganan, dan SPK pemasangan dibuat dan ditugaskan ke &lt;nama teknisi&gt;." (Customer, subscription and installation work order created and assigned to &lt;technician name&gt;.) The sentence about the access link appears only if the customer account is active, and it states what will happen: the email joins the sending queue.
4. If **Akun aktif** (Active account) is on, the customer receives the email **Akun pelanggan Anda sudah dibuat** (Your customer account has been created) with the button **Atur kata sandi** (Set password), valid for 60 minutes. The email body names your company ("Akun pelanggan Anda di &lt;perusahaan&gt; sudah dibuat" (Your customer account at &lt;company&gt; has been created)), while the sender and the email header remain Inbils because an email that contains a credential link always goes out through the platform sender. After the customer saves the password, the sign-in page shows "Kata sandi tersimpan. Masuk lewat aplikasi Inbils." (Password saved. Sign in through the Inbils app.) with the button **Buka aplikasi Inbils** (Open the Inbils app) (to the app page on Play Store if its address has been filled in), because the customer works in the customer app, not on the web. The registration toast does not check **Akun aktif**: if the account is inactive, the email is not sent, and the access link sentence does not appear in the toast. If the email is lost or the link has expired, open the customer details and click **Kirim ulang link akses** (Resend access link).
5. If the work order is not yet assigned, an admin or manager opens **Penugasan** ([section 3.1](/docs/guides/web/operations/dispatch/)), finds that **Menunggu penugasan** work order, and clicks **Tugaskan** (Assign). Select a technician in **Pilih teknisi lapangan** (Select field technician): each option reads "&lt;nama&gt; · &lt;n&gt; pekerjaan aktif" (&lt;name&gt; · &lt;n&gt; active jobs), and the technician with the fewest jobs is labelled "Paling sedikit pekerjaan" (Fewest jobs) when the workloads are not equal. Fill in **Tanggal kunjungan** if needed (optional; empty means the work order schedule does not change), then click **Simpan penugasan** (Save assignment). The toast "&lt;kode SPK&gt; ditugaskan ke &lt;nama teknisi&gt;." (&lt;SPK code&gt; assigned to &lt;technician name&gt;.) appears, and the work order status becomes **Ditugaskan**. The technician receives a WhatsApp message if the company WhatsApp is active. On the first assignment, the customer receives a schedule message if the work order has a **Tanggal kunjungan**, whether from registration or from this dialog. Without a date, no schedule message is sent.
6. If the work order must install a device or use items, an admin or manager opens that work order ([section 3.3](/docs/guides/web/operations/work-orders/)) and clicks **Tambah item** (Add item). Select a **Barang** (Items) of type **Aset** (Asset) and then an **Aset jaringan** (Network asset) that is **Tersedia**, or select a **Habis pakai** (Consumable) item and fill in **Jumlah terpakai** (Quantity used) if the quantity is already known. Work order items do not reserve stock. If materials are taken from the warehouse through an item request, follow workflow 9.3.
7. The technician works on the work order in the technician app: starts the job (status **Dikerjakan** (In progress)), records the materials used, uploads evidence photos, then submits the result (status **Menunggu review** (Awaiting review)). If the technician starts farther than **Jarak check-in maksimal (meter)** (Maximum check-in distance (metres)) ([section 8.1](/docs/guides/web/administration/company/)) from the customer location, the technician must write a reason, and the work order page shows it in the **Check-in** row as "Jauh dari lokasi: &lt;alasan&gt;" (Far from location: &lt;reason&gt;). The submission is rejected with the message "Bukti pekerjaan wajib diunggah sebelum SPK diajukan." (Work evidence must be uploaded before the work order is submitted.) if there is no evidence yet. The submission is also rejected if an item request with status **Diserahkan** (Handed over) still has leftover material that has not been reported ([section 6.8](/docs/guides/web/inventory/material-returns/)).
8. A manager or admin who is neither the creator of the work order nor its technician opens **Persetujuan** (Approvals) ([section 3.2](/docs/guides/web/operations/approvals/)), clicks **Tinjau** (Review), then **Setujui** (Approve). Approval needs at least one piece of evidence and cannot be undone. If the work is not good enough, click **Tolak** (Reject) and write the reason: the work order gets status **Dikembalikan** (Returned) (not "Ditolak" (Rejected)), and the technician receives that reason.
9. When approved, the work order becomes **Selesai** (Completed) and Inbils runs these steps in one transaction:
   - Stock of the materials recorded in **Jumlah terpakai** decreases at the work order **Lokasi**, and **Mutasi Stok** (Stock movements) records it as **Keluar** (Out) ([section 6.7](/docs/guides/web/inventory/stock-movements/)). Quantities that already left through an item request are not deducted twice.
   - On an **Instalasi** work order, the selected asset becomes **Terpasang** (Installed) at the customer and subscription ([section 5.2](/docs/guides/web/network/network-assets/)).
   - The **Menunggu** subscription becomes **Aktif** (Active), and the customer status becomes **Aktif**.
   - If the work order has an installation fee or materials selected for billing, Inbils creates a one-time invoice. The fee line is named **Biaya pemasangan** (Installation fee). Materials become a **Bahan terpakai** (Materials used) line only if the switch is on.
10. If **Kirim tagihan pemasangan otomatis** (Send installation invoice automatically) is on, the customer sees the invoice at once. If it is off, open the card **Tagihan draf menunggu dikirim** (Draft invoices waiting to be sent) on the Finance Dashboard, check the draft, then click **Kirim tagihan** (Send invoice). Holders of `billing.send` also receive a bell notification and a push notification.
11. For monthly invoices, click **Buat tagihan bulanan** (Create monthly invoices) in **Tagihan** (Invoices), select the **Periode** (Period), click **Lihat pratinjau** (View preview), then **Buat &lt;n&gt; tagihan** (Create &lt;n&gt; invoices). Issuing also runs on a daily schedule. Invoices get status **Belum dibayar** (Unpaid) at once, and the first month is calculated pro rata from the activation date. A subscription that is **Menunggu** or **Ditangguhkan** (Suspended) is not included in monthly invoices.
12. The customer pays through the customer app. The payment is applied to the invoice after Midtrans confirms it. Holders of the `billing.payment.record` permission (admin, manager and staff) can also record a payment: open an invoice that is not fully paid (**Belum dibayar**, **Dibayar sebagian** (Partially paid), or **Lewat jatuh tempo** (Overdue)), click **Catat pembayaran** (Record payment), fill in **Jumlah** (Amount) (not more than the remainder) and **Metode** (Method) (**Tunai** (Cash), **Transfer** (Transfer), **Cek** (Cheque), or **Lainnya** (Other)), fill in **Referensi** (Reference) if there is one, then click **Simpan** (Save). The invoice becomes **Dibayar sebagian** or **Lunas** (Paid). A **Lunas** invoice has the button **Unduh kuitansi** (Download receipt) for holders of `billing.pdf.view`.
13. If an invoice is not paid past its due date, a scheduled job marks it **Lewat jatuh tempo**, and the invoice list already shows it as such once the due date has passed. The customer also receives a WhatsApp reminder three days and one day before the due date, and one day after it. Holders of `billing.send` can send a reminder themselves with the button **Kirim pengingat** (Send reminder) in **Tunggakan** (Arrears), tab **Per tagihan** (Per invoice) ([section 7.2](/docs/guides/web/finance-and-reports/arrears/)), at most once a day for one invoice. If the switch **Tangguhkan langganan yang menunggak** (Suspend overdue subscriptions) is on in **Pengaturan Perusahaan** (Company settings) ([section 8.1](/docs/guides/web/administration/company/)), a subscription whose invoice passes the **Batas keterlambatan** (Lateness limit) is put in isolir (internet blocked while the account is suspended) automatically and gets status **Ditangguhkan**. A payment that clears all arrears reactivates a subscription that was automatically put in isolir, if the switch **Aktifkan kembali saat tagihan lunas** (Reactivate when invoices are paid) is on. Default values: suspension off, limit 7 days, reactivation on. Manual suspension and internet blocking are never lifted automatically. Admins and managers can also isolate or block by hand from **Tunggakan** ([section 7.2](/docs/guides/web/finance-and-reports/arrears/) and [5.3](/docs/guides/web/network/nas-routers/)).

The end result: the subscription is **Aktif** and the invoice is **Lunas**. That customer's invoices and payments are also counted in **Metrik Bisnis** (Business metrics) and **KPI Penjualan** (Sales KPIs) ([section 7.4](/docs/guides/web/finance-and-reports/business-metrics/) and [7.10](/docs/guides/web/finance-and-reports/sales-kpis/)).

## Common errors

- The **Buat Pelanggan** button is missing: your role does not hold all the registration permissions, which are `customer.create`, `customer.address.manage`, `customer.subscription.manage`, and `spk.create`. Ask an admin or manager to register the customer, or add the permissions in Roles ([section 8.6](/docs/guides/web/administration/roles/)).
- You registered a customer and now cannot approve its work order. This is the separation of duties rule and it applies to admins too. In **Persetujuan**, that work order has no **Tinjau** button, and the **Tindakan** (Actions) column reads "Perlu penyetuju lain" (Another approver needed). Ask a colleague who is allowed to approve it.
- The subscription is still **Menunggu** although the technician has finished: the work order is not yet approved. Open **Persetujuan**.
- Stock does not decrease and the asset is not installed after approval: the work order has no **Lokasi**. A work order from registration always has a **Lokasi**, but a manual one may not. A work order that is still **Draf** (Draft) or **Menunggu penugasan** can be changed with the **Ubah** (Edit) button on the work order page ([section 3.3](/docs/guides/web/operations/work-orders/)), including its **Lokasi**. After it is assigned, the work order can no longer be changed with that button.
- The installation invoice does not appear after approval: the work order has no installation fee or materials enabled for billing. If the invoice exists but the customer has not seen it yet, check the card **Tagihan draf menunggu dikirim** and the switch **Kirim tagihan pemasangan otomatis**.
- The customer cannot sign in to the app: **Akun aktif** was off at registration so the email was not sent, or the link is more than 60 minutes old. Activate the customer in Customers ([section 4.1](/docs/guides/web/customers-and-services/customers/)) and the user in Users ([section 8.5](/docs/guides/web/administration/users/)), then click **Kirim ulang link akses**. If either is inactive, Inbils refuses with the message "Customer atau akun portalnya nonaktif, tautan akses tidak dikirim." (The customer or their portal account is inactive, access link not sent.) This button is limited to three times per minute.
- The customer does not receive WhatsApp: open the **WhatsApp** card in the customer details. The **Status** and **Keterangan** (Remarks) columns state why the message was skipped, for example "Pelanggan belum setuju" (Customer has not agreed) or "WhatsApp perusahaan nonaktif" (Company WhatsApp is inactive).
- A payment fails with the message "Jumlah pembayaran melebihi sisa tagihan." (The payment amount exceeds the invoice remainder.) or "Referensi pembayaran sudah ada." (The payment reference already exists.): reduce the **Jumlah** or use another reference ([section 7.1](/docs/guides/web/finance-and-reports/invoices/)).
