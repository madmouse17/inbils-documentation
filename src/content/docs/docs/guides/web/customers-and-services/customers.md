title: "4.1 Customers (Pelanggan)"
description: "Add, view, edit and deactivate customers, manage their addresses, contacts, documents and subscriptions, and resend access links."
---

The **Pelanggan** (Customers) menu stores customer identity, addresses, contacts, the staff member who acquired the customer (**Diperoleh oleh**, Acquired by), subscriptions and service history. Use it to onboard new customers and to review the whole relationship with a customer.

Roles that can open the list: admin, manager and staff. Basic permission: Lihat pelanggan (View customers, `customer.view`). The built-in roles have these limits:

| Tindakan (Actions) | Permissions | Built-in roles |
|---|---|---|
| View customers | Lihat pelanggan (View customers, `customer.view`) | Admin, manager, staff |
| Full onboarding | Buat pelanggan (Create customers, `customer.create`), Kelola alamat pelanggan (Manage customer addresses, `customer.address.manage`), Kelola langganan (Manage subscriptions, `customer.subscription.manage`), Buat SPK (Create work order, `spk.create`) | Admin, manager |
| Assign a technician when registering a customer | Tugaskan SPK (Assign work order, `spk.assign`) | Admin, manager |
| Edit customers, send access links, and upload and delete customer documents | Ubah pelanggan (Edit customers, `customer.update`) | Admin, manager, staff |
| Manage addresses and contacts | Kelola alamat pelanggan (`customer.address.manage`) | Admin, manager, staff |
| Delete customers | Hapus pelanggan (Delete customers, `customer.delete`) | Admin |
| Export customers | Ekspor pelanggan (Export customers, `customer.export`) | Admin, manager |
| Set **Diperoleh oleh** | Tetapkan pemeroleh pelanggan (Set customer acquirer, `customer.sales.assign`) | Admin, manager, staff |
| Correct **Diperoleh oleh** | Koreksi pemeroleh pelanggan (Correct customer acquirer, `customer.sales.correct`) | Admin, manager |
| View subscriptions | Lihat langganan (View subscriptions, `customer.subscription.view`) | Admin, manager, staff |
| Create subscriptions and run the subscription lifecycle | Kelola langganan (`customer.subscription.manage`) and the related action permissions | Admin, manager |
| View, manage and reveal the passwords of network accounts | Lihat akun jaringan (View network accounts, `network_asset.account.view`), Kelola akun jaringan (Manage network accounts, `network_asset.account.manage`), Tampilkan kata sandi akun jaringan (Show network account password, `network_asset.account.reveal`) | Admin, manager, staff |

An admin can change the permission set through **Peran** (Roles) and **Hak Akses** (Permissions). The buttons you see on a custom role can differ from this table.

## Opening and reading the list

1. In the sidebar, open the group **Pelanggan & Layanan** (Customers & Services), then click **Pelanggan**.
2. Type a name, code or phone number in the **Cari** (Search) box. The list updates a moment after you stop typing. Filters apply as soon as you choose them, with no **Filter** button on a wide screen:
   - **Tipe** (Type): **Semua tipe** (All types), **Perorangan** (Individual) or **Perusahaan** (Company).
   - **Status**: **Semua status** (All statuses), **Prospek** (Prospect), **Aktif** (Active), **Nonaktif** (Inactive), **Ditangguhkan** (Suspended) or **Berhenti** (Terminated).
   - **Akun** (Account): **Semua akun** (All accounts), **Aktif** or **Nonaktif**. This filter works on the manual **Akun aktif** (Account active) switch in the customer data. Its value differs from the service status.
   - **Diperoleh oleh**: a search box. Type to search, then choose. The options are **Belum ditentukan** (Not set) or an employee name. An employee who is now inactive is written with "(nonaktif)" ((inactive)). Click **Hapus pilihan** (Clear selection) to empty the filter. This filter is visible only with Tetapkan pemeroleh pelanggan or Koreksi pemeroleh pelanggan.
   - **Reset filter** (Reset filters) appears when a search or filter is active. Click it to clear everything.
3. On a narrow screen (phone), the list appears as cards and the customer name stays a link to the details. The filters move to a **Filter** button, which adds the number of active choices in brackets when there are any. The button opens the **Filter pelanggan** (Customer filters) sheet with the buttons **Reset filter** and **Lihat hasil** (View results).
4. Read the columns **Kode** (Code), **Nama** (Name), **Tipe**, **Telepon** (Phone), **Status**, **Diperoleh oleh**, **Alamat** (Address), **Langganan** (Subscriptions) and **Aksi** (Actions). The **Alamat** and **Langganan** columns hold counts. The code, name, type, phone and status columns can be sorted. The default order uses the code from smallest to largest.

   ![Customer list with the Cari box, the Tipe, Status, Akun and Diperoleh oleh filters, the Ekspor and Buat Pelanggan buttons, and the columns Kode to Aksi](~/assets/guide/web/customers-index.webp)

5. The customer name is a link to the details. The **Aksi** column holds an **Ubah** (Edit) button (with Ubah pelanggan; without that permission the button is **Lihat**, View) and the **Aksi lainnya** (More actions) menu with **Lihat detail** (View details) and **Hapus** (Delete, with Hapus pelanggan).
6. Use **Ekspor** (Export) to download **Ekspor CSV** (Export CSV) or **Ekspor PDF** (Export PDF) with the active filters. This menu appears with Ekspor pelanggan. The export holds at most 5,000 rows by default. Narrow the filters if the result is cut.
7. Below the table, read the row count summary and choose **Baris per halaman** (Rows per page) (10, 25 or 50). Move between pages with **Sebelumnya** (Previous) and **Berikutnya** (Next). When there is no result, the list shows **Belum ada pelanggan** (No customers yet) (without filters) or **Tidak ada pelanggan yang cocok** (No matching customers) (with filters), with a **Reset filter** button.
8. Tell these two indicators apart:

   | Indicator | Value | Meaning |
   |---|---|---|
   | The **Akun** filter and the **Akun nonaktif** (Account inactive) badge in the **Status** column | **Aktif**, **Nonaktif** | The manual **Akun aktif** switch in the customer data. The **Akun nonaktif** badge appears only when the switch is off. |
   | **Status** | **Prospek** | There is no subscription yet, or all are still **Menunggu** (Pending) |
   | **Status** | **Aktif** | There is an **Aktif** subscription |
   | **Status** | **Ditangguhkan** | None is **Aktif**, but one is **Ditangguhkan** |
   | **Status** | **Berhenti** | All subscriptions are **Berhenti** |
   | **Status** | **Nonaktif** | Any other combination, for example **Menunggu** and **Berhenti** |

   The service status is calculated automatically from the subscriptions. You do not fill it in by hand.

## Adding a new customer

1. Click **Buat Pelanggan** (Create customer). The button appears only when all the full onboarding permissions are available. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (*).

   ![Buat Pelanggan form on the Data pelanggan tab, with the tabs Alamat, Kontak and Langganan & SPK](~/assets/guide/web/customers-create.webp)

2. On the **Data pelanggan** (Customer data) tab, fill in the identity data:
   - **Kode**: the system creates the code when you save. With automatic numbering, the box is locked and shows the expected code. If manual numbering is enabled in Penomoran (Numbering, [section 7.13](/docs/guides/web/finance-and-reports/numbering/)), you can leave the field empty for an automatic code or fill in up to 50 characters. The code must be unique within the company.
   - **Nama**: required, up to 255 characters.
   - **Tipe**: choose **Perorangan** or **Perusahaan**. The default is **Perorangan**.
   - **Email**: required, e-mail format, up to 255 characters, and unique across all user accounts. This e-mail becomes the customer's sign-in e-mail.
   - **Telepon**: required, up to 50 characters. The field accepts only digits and the characters `(`, `)`, `+` and `-`. Other characters disappear as you type. The field shows a hint that the link to set the password is sent to the customer's e-mail.
   - **NPWP**: required for **Perusahaan**, optional for **Perorangan**, up to 100 characters.
   - **Narahubung** (Contact person): required for **Perusahaan**, optional for **Perorangan**, up to 255 characters. This value becomes the sign-in account name. If it is empty, the system uses **Nama**.
   - **Akun aktif**: on by default. If you turn it off, the system still creates the sign-in account in an inactive state and does not send the access link.
   - **Catatan** (Notes): optional.
3. Set the consent and the acquirer on the same tab:
   - **Pelanggan setuju menerima notifikasi WhatsApp** (Customer agrees to receive WhatsApp notifications): not ticked by default. Tick it only after the customer agrees to receive invoice issued, due date reminder, payment received, ticket status and technician visit schedule messages. Sending still needs the company's WhatsApp feature to be active and a valid Indonesian mobile number.
   - **Diperoleh oleh**: appears with Tetapkan pemeroleh pelanggan. Type to search for an employee, then choose, or leave it empty (**Belum ditentukan**). Click **Hapus pilihan** to empty it again. The options hold only active accounts in the company that have an active employee profile and hold Buat pelanggan. If the creator qualifies, their name is filled in as the default. If no employee matches, the box shows **Tidak ada karyawan yang memenuhi syarat.** (No eligible employees.)
4. Open the **Alamat** tab. Provide at least one address and choose exactly one main address and one installation address. The first address is already both. Each address is a numbered card (the heading **Alamat** followed by a sequence number):
   - **Label**: required, up to 100 characters, and must not equal the label of another address of this customer. Examples in the field: Rumah / Kantor / Pemasangan (Home / Office / Installation).
   - Map: click the map, drag the red pin, or use the search box above the map. The **Alamat** button searches for a location by text and needs at least 3 characters. Click **Cari**, then choose one of the results. If the **Alamat** (street) field on the same card is still empty, or still holds a street from an earlier search result, the result you choose fills it with the street name and house number (if OpenStreetMap has that street). An address you typed yourself is not overwritten. The **Lintang / Bujur** (Latitude / Longitude) button opens two boxes for typing coordinates. Click **Cari** to place the pin. The **Lokasi saat ini** (Current location) button uses the location of your device. If the search result is empty, the map shows **Lokasi tidak ditemukan. Coba alamat yang lebih spesifik.** (Location not found. Try a more specific address.)
   - **Lintang** and **Bujur**: required and filled by the map. You cannot type in the boxes directly. Latitude must be between -90 and 90. Longitude must be between -180 and 180.
   - **Provinsi** (Province), **Kota / Kabupaten** (City / Regency), **Kecamatan** (District) and **Kelurahan / Desa** (Village): required. Each box is a search box (type to search, then choose). Choose them in order. The next box becomes active only after the box above it is chosen. The combination must form a valid area hierarchy. Choosing a map search result also fills in the area and the postal code.
   - **Alamat**: required. **Kode pos** (Postal code) and **Catatan** are optional.
   - **Alamat utama** (Main address) and **Alamat pemasangan** (Installation address): choose one card for each marker. Turning a marker on for one card moves it from the old card.
   - Click **Tambah alamat** (Add address) to add a card. The **Hapus** button on a card is inactive when there is only one address. Deleting a card that holds a marker moves the marker to the first remaining address.
5. Open the **Kontak** (Contacts) tab. Provide at least one contact and choose exactly one main contact. The main contact card (the heading **Kontak** followed by a sequence number) starts with the switch **Sama dengan data pelanggan** (Same as customer data), which is on at first. While it is on, the main contact uses the contact person (or **Nama** if the contact person is empty), **Telepon** and **Email** from the **Data pelanggan** tab, so the same data is not typed twice. Changing a field on that card, choosing another main contact, or deleting the main contact turns the switch off. The fields that were already copied stay for you to edit. Turning the switch on again makes the customer data replace those fields.
   - **Nama**: required, up to 255 characters.
   - **Jabatan** (Position): optional, up to 255 characters.
   - **Telepon**: required during onboarding, up to 50 characters, with digits and `(`, `)`, `+` or `-`.
   - **Email**: required during onboarding, e-mail format, up to 255 characters.
   - **Catatan**: optional.
   - **Kontak utama** (Main contact): choose one card. Click **Tambah kontak** (Add contact) to add a contact. The **Hapus** button is inactive when there is only one contact.
6. Open the **Langganan & SPK** (Subscription & work order (SPK)) tab:
   - **Paket layanan** (Service package): required. A search box (type to search, then choose). The options hold only active packages from active categories, grouped under the category name as the heading. Each option shows the package name with the package code and the price per month. Changing **Tipe** reorders the groups: **Perorangan** shows **Rumahan** (Residential) on top, **Perusahaan** shows **Bisnis** (Business) then **Korporat** (Corporate). All packages can still be chosen for any type. Choosing a package fills **Biaya bulanan** (Monthly fee), **Biaya pemasangan** (Installation fee) and **Masa kontrak (bulan)** (Contract period (months)) with the package values. You can change them.
   - **POP pelayan** (Serving POP): required. A search box. The options hold only active locations in the company, written with the location code and name.
   - **Port drop (ODP)**: optional and appears only with Tetapkan port ODN (Assign ODN port, `odn.assign`). The box becomes active only after **POP pelayan** is chosen (before that it shows **Pilih POP layanan untuk melihat port kosongnya.** (Choose a service POP to see its free ports.)). Type at least 2 letters of the ODP code or splitter label, then choose a free port under that POP. Each option shows the ODP code, the splitter ratio or label, and the port number, with a remarks line holding the ODP name, the number of free ports out of all ports of that ODP (for example "5/8 port kosong" (5/8 ports free)), the distance of the ODP from the installation address ("850 m" or "1,4 km"), and the location path of the ODP. If the address marked for installation already has coordinates, the nearest ODP appears on top. Without coordinates, the distance is not written and the order follows the ODP code. If there is no port, the box shows **Tidak ada port kosong yang cocok di bawah POP ini.** (No matching free ports under this POP.) Changing the POP clears the port choice.
   - **Tanggal tagihan (1-28)** (Billing date (1-28)): required, a whole number from 1 to 28. The default is 1.
   - **Masa kontrak (bulan)**: optional, a whole number of at least 1. Leave it empty to use the minimum period of the package.
   - **Biaya bulanan** and **Biaya pemasangan**: optional, a number of at least 0. Leave them empty to use the package price.
   - **Catatan langganan** (Subscription notes): optional.
   - Check the summary at the bottom: **Alamat pemasangan** (the label of the address marked for installation) and **Status SPK setelah dibuat** (Work order status after creation), which is **Menunggu penugasan** (Awaiting assignment), or **Ditugaskan** (Assigned) if you choose a technician in the next block.
   - **Tugaskan sekarang** (Assign now): a block in the same summary card, with the remark "Opsional. Isi bila pemasangan sudah disepakati; kosongkan untuk menugaskan nanti dari antrean Penugasan." (Optional. Fill in if the installation is agreed; leave empty to assign later from the Dispatch queue.) With Tugaskan SPK (`spk.assign`), the block holds **Teknisi** (Technician) and **Tanggal kunjungan** (Visit date), both optional. **Teknisi** is a search box (**Cari teknisi**, Search technician) with active technicians. Each option shows the name and the number of active jobs ("... · 2 pekerjaan aktif" (... · 2 active jobs)), and the technician with the fewest jobs is labelled **Paling sedikit pekerjaan** (Fewest jobs). If none matches, the box shows "Tidak ada teknisi aktif yang cocok." (No matching active technician.) **Tanggal kunjungan** must not be before today and becomes the schedule of the installation work order. Without Tugaskan SPK, the block is titled **Jadwal kunjungan** (Visit schedule) with the remark "Opsional. Kosongkan bila belum disepakati." (Optional. Leave empty if not yet agreed.) and holds only **Tanggal kunjungan**. The chosen technician is assigned in the same transaction as the customer creation, so if the assignment is rejected, no customer is saved. If **Teknisi** is empty, assign later through the Penugasan queue ([section 3.1](/docs/guides/web/operations/dispatch/)).

   ![Langganan & SPK tab on the Buat Pelanggan form, with the Status SPK setelah dibuat summary and the Tugaskan sekarang block](~/assets/guide/web/customers-create-assign.webp)

7. Click **Buat pelanggan dan SPK** (Create customer and work order). If validation fails, the screen moves to the tab that holds the problem and the toast **Data gagal disimpan. Periksa input.** (Data could not be saved. Check the input.) appears. The message for each field shows below the field. Click **Batal** (Cancel) to return to the list without saving.
8. The system saves the whole onboarding in one transaction:
   - the customer, addresses, contacts, WhatsApp consent and acquirer;
   - a user account with the role `customer`, joined to the company, with the e-mail already marked as verified, and linked to the customer;
   - a new subscription with the status **Menunggu**, the ODP port if chosen, and an installation work order with the status **Menunggu penugasan** (or **Ditugaskan** if you chose a technician) with the priority **Sedang** (Medium) and the visit schedule if filled in. The work order title has the form **Pemasangan** (Installation) followed by the subscription code and the customer name;
   - the onboarding audit record.
9. There is no password field on the form. The system creates a random 32-character password that is never shown. The phone number is not a password.
10. If **Akun aktif** is on, the system queues an e-mail titled **Akun pelanggan Anda sudah dibuat** (Your customer account has been created) after the transaction completes. The **Atur kata sandi** (Set password) button is valid for 60 minutes. The customer sets the password through that link, then signs in to the Inbils Pelanggan app with that e-mail.
11. After success, the screen opens the details of the new customer. The toast says "Pelanggan, langganan, dan SPK pemasangan dibuat" (Customer, subscription and installation work order created), then "dan ditugaskan ke {nama teknisi}." (and assigned to {technician name}.) if you chose a technician, or just a full stop if not. If **Akun aktif** is on, the toast adds "Tautan akses akan dikirim ke &lt;email&gt;." (The access link will be sent to &lt;email&gt;.) That sentence is a promise: the e-mail enters the queue and is sent when the queue worker processes it. The customer and subscription codes follow the Penomoran settings ([section 7.13](/docs/guides/web/finance-and-reports/numbering/)).
12. The installation work order is recorded as created by you. The system forbids the creator of a work order from approving it (message **Pembuat SPK tidak boleh menyetujui SPK sendiri.** (The work order creator must not approve their own work order.)). Ask another authorised person to approve this work order in SPK ([section 3.3](/docs/guides/web/operations/work-orders/)).

## Opening customer details

1. In the list, click the customer name or choose **Aksi lainnya**, then **Lihat detail**. The **Kembali** (Back) button in the top corner takes you to the list.

   ![Customer details with the buttons Kembali, Kirim ulang link akses and Ubah, and the cards Data pelanggan, Status and WhatsApp](~/assets/guide/web/customers-show.webp)

2. Read the **Data pelanggan** card for e-mail, phone, NPWP, contact person, **Diperoleh oleh** and notes. Notes appear only when filled in. The **Status** card shows the type, the service status and the **Akun** status (**Aktif** or **Nonaktif**).
3. Read the **WhatsApp** card. The badge shows **Setuju sejak** (Agreed since) (with a date), **Berhenti sejak** (Stopped since) (with a date) or **Belum setuju** (Not agreed yet). If the phone number is not a valid Indonesian mobile number, the card shows the warning **Nomor telepon pelanggan bukan nomor seluler Indonesia yang valid.** (The customer phone number is not a valid Indonesian mobile number.) The table holds the 20 latest messages with the columns **Pesan** (Message), **Subjek** (Subject), **Status**, **Keterangan** (Remarks), **Nomor** (Number) and **Waktu** (Time). The number shows only the last four digits. The message status is a badge: **Antrean** (Queued), **Terkirim** (Sent), **Diterima** (Received), **Dibaca** (Read), **Gagal** (Failed) or **Dilewati** (Skipped). A skipped message writes its reason in **Keterangan**, for example **Pelanggan belum setuju** (Customer has not agreed).
4. Open the tabs **Alamat**, **Kontak**, **Langganan** and **Dokumen** (Documents) (each followed by a count) for a summary of the related data. The tabs **Tagihan** (Invoices), **SPK** and **Tiket** (Tickets) appear when the user has the permission of the related module. The invoice, work order and ticket histories each show at most the 50 latest records. The **Dokumen** tab is explained in the next section.
5. The **Langganan** tab on the details is a summary (**Kode**, **Paket** (Package), **Status**, **Biaya bulanan**, **Tanggal tagih** (Billing date)). Users with Lihat langganan (`customer.subscription.view`) also see an **Aksi** column with a **Kelola langganan** (Manage subscription) link, and the subscription code becomes a link. Both open the subscription details (see "Mengelola langganan pelanggan", Managing customer subscriptions). Without that permission the code is plain text. The **Alamat** and **Kontak** tabs are read only. Add or change their data through **Ubah**.
6. The **Ubah** button always shows on the details. Without Ubah pelanggan, clicking the button opens the **Akses ditolak** (Access denied) page.

## Managing customer documents

The **Dokumen** tab stores files that belong to the customer, for example an ID card or a contract. The tab title is followed by the number of files, for example **Dokumen (2)**. All users who can open the customer details see the list. The upload form and the **Hapus** button exist only with Ubah pelanggan (`customer.update`).

1. Open the customer details, then click the **Dokumen** tab. If there is no file yet, the tab shows **Belum ada dokumen. Unggah KTP atau kontrak pelanggan.** (No documents yet. Upload the customer's ID card or contract.)

   ![Dokumen tab on the customer details with the fields Jenis dokumen and Berkas, and the Unggah button](~/assets/guide/web/customers-show-documents.webp)

2. Choose **Jenis dokumen** (Document type) (the first option is **Pilih jenis** (Choose type)). The options are the active document types created in **Jenis Dokumen** (Document types, [section 8.8](/docs/guides/web/administration/documents/)) that apply to customers, that is, those whose **Berlaku untuk** (Applies to) column is empty, **Customer** or **Pelanggan**. A type that has a validity period is written with its number of days, that is, the type name followed by the number of days in brackets.
3. Choose **Berkas** (File). The format is PDF, JPG or PNG with a size of at most 10 MB (hint "PDF, JPG, atau PNG. Maksimum 10 MB." (PDF, JPG or PNG. Maximum 10 MB.)). The **Unggah** (Upload) button becomes active after the type and the file are chosen.
4. Click **Unggah**. The toast **Dokumen diunggah.** (Document uploaded.) appears and the file enters the table. If the type has a validity period, the expiry date is the upload day, by the company calendar, plus the number of days of that type.
5. Read the table with the columns **Dokumen** (the type name, with the file name below it), **Berlaku** (Valid), **Diunggah** (Uploaded) (date and time) and **Aksi**. The **Berlaku** column shows **Berlaku sampai {tanggal}** (Valid until {date}), or **Tanpa masa berlaku** (No validity period) if the type has no validity period. The badge **Segera habis** (Expiring soon) appears when the expiry date falls within the next 30 days, and the badge **Kedaluwarsa** (Expired) when the date has passed.
6. Click **Lihat** to open the file in a new tab. Click **Hapus** to delete it: the dialog **Hapus {nama berkas}?** (Delete {file name}?) says "Berkas ini akan dihapus permanen dari data pelanggan." (This file will be permanently deleted from the customer data.) Click **Hapus dokumen** (Delete document), and the toast **Dokumen dihapus.** (Document deleted.) appears. Uploads and deletions are recorded in the Log Audit (Audit log, [section 7.12](/docs/guides/web/finance-and-reports/audit-log/)).

Every day at 00:30 UTC, the scheduler looks for documents that expire from today up to 30 days ahead, with the day taken from each company's calendar. For each document, all active users of that company who hold Ubah pelanggan receive one bell notification titled "Dokumen {jenis} {nama pelanggan} segera habis" ({type} document of {customer name} expires soon) with the text "Berlaku sampai {tanggal}." (Valid until {date}.) The reminder is sent once for each expiry date, so the same document is not reminded again every day. The queue and the scheduler must run on the server. Contact the server administrator if the reminder does not appear.

## Resending the access link

1. Open the customer details, then click **Kirim ulang link akses** (Resend access link). The button needs Ubah pelanggan (`customer.update`).
2. Make sure the customer has a sign-in account with an e-mail. Make sure the customer status and the user account are both active. If not, a red toast appears with one of these messages: **Customer belum punya akun portal beremail, tautan akses tidak dikirim.** (The customer has no portal account with an e-mail, so the access link is not sent.) or **Customer atau akun portalnya nonaktif, tautan akses tidak dikirim.** (The customer or their portal account is inactive, so the access link is not sent.)
3. The system queues an e-mail with the link to the sign-in account e-mail and shows the toast **Tautan akses akan dikirim ke &lt;email&gt;.** The e-mail is sent when the queue worker processes it. The limit is 3 requests per minute. The fourth request is rejected with HTTP 429.
4. A new link cancels the old link. The link is valid for 60 minutes. After setting the password, the customer uses the Inbils Pelanggan app.
5. If the customer has already agreed to WhatsApp, the system also queues a notice that the link is in the e-mail. The WhatsApp message carries no link or token. This notice is limited to once per account every hour. The message appears in the WhatsApp table on the details as **Info link akses portal** (Portal access link info).

## Editing customers, addresses and contacts

1. From the details, click **Ubah**. You can also click **Ubah** on a list row. The **Ubah Pelanggan** (Edit Customer) page has three tabs: **Data pelanggan**, **Alamat** and **Kontak**. The last two are followed by their record counts. The **Data pelanggan** tab uses the same fields as the add form. **Kode** is locked ("Dibuat otomatis dan tidak dapat diubah." (Created automatically and cannot be changed.)) unless manual numbering is active. With manual numbering, the code is required and must be unique.

   ![Ubah Pelanggan page with the tabs Data pelanggan, Alamat and Kontak and the customer data fields](~/assets/guide/web/customers-edit.webp)

2. Edit the **Data pelanggan** tab, then click **Simpan pelanggan** (Save customer). The toast **Pelanggan diperbarui.** (Customer updated.) appears and the screen returns to the customer list. Click **Batal** to discard the changes and return to the details. The **Kembali** button in the top corner also returns to the details. The **Alamat** and **Kontak** tabs save their own data, so unsaved changes on the **Data pelanggan** tab are neither saved nor lost while you manage addresses and contacts.
3. Users with Ubah pelanggan can change the tick box **Pelanggan setuju menerima notifikasi WhatsApp**. Below it, **Tercatat:** (Recorded:) is shown, followed by **Setuju sejak** (with a date), **Berhenti sejak** (with a date) or **Belum ada persetujuan tercatat** (No consent recorded yet). Ticking records the time, the source `staff`, and the recording user. Unticking records the stop time. The system only sends the consent if you change it.
4. If the number cannot be normalised as an Indonesian mobile number, the screen shows the warning **Nomor pelanggan ini bukan nomor seluler Indonesia yang valid** (This customer number is not a valid Indonesian mobile number). WhatsApp messages are skipped until the number is corrected.
5. A customer can reply `STOP` or `BERHENTI`, in upper or lower case. The system revokes the consent of the customer with a matching number only in the company that owns that sending WhatsApp number (see **Integrasi**, [section 8.9](/docs/guides/web/administration/integrations/)). Other companies that use a different sending number are not affected, even if the customer has the same phone number. Staff can tick the box again after obtaining new consent.
6. On the **Alamat** tab, each address appears as a row with the mark **(utama)** (main) or **(pemasangan)** (installation), an **Ubah** button and a **Hapus** button. The **Tambah alamat** button empties the form below the list. The form is titled **Tambah Alamat** (Add Address) (button **Tambah**, Add) or **Ubah Alamat** (Edit Address) (button **Simpan**), with the same fields as on the **Alamat** tab of the add form: the map, the tiered area, **Alamat**, **Kode pos**, **Catatan** and the two markers. Click **Batal** to empty the form. Toasts: **Alamat ditambahkan.** (Address added.), **Alamat diperbarui.** (Address updated.) or **Alamat dihapus permanen.** (Address permanently deleted.) The first address is always the main and installation address. The system keeps exactly one main address and one installation address. Deleting an address that carries a marker moves the marker to the oldest remaining address.
7. An address used by a subscription cannot be deleted. The **Hapus** button on an address opens the dialog "Hapus alamat" (Delete address) with the text "Alamat yang belum pernah dipakai langganan dihapus permanen." (An address never used by a subscription is deleted permanently.) If the address is already used, the dialog stays open and writes the reason in red text, for example "Alamat masih dipakai 1 langganan, jadi tidak bisa dihapus." (The address is still used by 1 subscription, so it cannot be deleted.) Changing the installation marker does not change the installation address of old subscriptions. An address that is permanently deleted cannot be restored.
8. On the **Kontak** tab, the form works the same way: **Tambah kontak**, **Ubah** or **Hapus**, the form **Tambah Kontak** (Add Contact) or **Ubah Kontak** (Edit Contact), and the toasts **Kontak ditambahkan.** (Contact added.), **Kontak diperbarui.** (Contact updated.) or **Kontak dihapus.** (Contact deleted.) **Nama** and **Telepon** are marked as required. **Jabatan**, **Email** and **Catatan** are optional. The main contact card is marked **(utama)**. If the main contact is deleted, the system chooses the oldest remaining contact as the main contact.
9. Users without Kelola alamat pelanggan see only the list of addresses and contacts, without buttons and forms.
10. The e-mail and the status of the customer on this page are not synchronised to the sign-in account created during onboarding. An admin changes the e-mail or status of the user account in Pengguna (Users, [section 8.5](/docs/guides/web/administration/users/)). **Kirim ulang link akses** always uses the e-mail of the user account.

## Managing sales attribution

1. **Diperoleh oleh** is visible on the list, the filters, the details, the edit page and the export only with Tetapkan pemeroleh pelanggan or Koreksi pemeroleh pelanggan.
2. For a customer who is **Belum ditentukan**, users with Tetapkan pemeroleh pelanggan can choose an eligible employee on the **Ubah Pelanggan** page. Type to search, then choose.
3. For a customer who already has an acquirer, changing or clearing it needs Koreksi pemeroleh pelanggan. Users without that permission see the acquirer name only as text.

The edit page shows **Saat ini diperoleh oleh:** (Currently acquired by:) followed by the name, and explains that you need to choose an eligible employee or empty the field to correct it.

## Managing customer subscriptions

1. The customer subscription list page is at `/admin/customers/<id>/subscriptions`. From the customer details, the **Kelola langganan** link in the **Langganan** tab opens the details of one subscription, not this list. The list opens through the **Kembali** button on the subscription details.
2. The **Langganan** page shows the customer code and name below the title. The page offers a **Cari** box (package code or name), the filters **Paket** (a search box, first option **Semua paket** (All packages)) and **Status** (**Semua status**, **Menunggu**, **Aktif**, **Ditangguhkan**, **Berhenti**), **Reset filter**, and the columns **Kode**, **Paket**, **Status**, **Biaya bulanan**, **Tanggal tagihan** and **Aksi** (a **Lihat** button). The subscription code is a link to the details. The code and status columns can be sorted. The **Ekspor** menu appears with Ekspor langganan (Export subscriptions, `customer.subscription.export`). The **Kembali** button opens the customer details.
3. Click **Buat Langganan** (Create Subscription). The button shows for all users of this page, but the server accepts a save only with Kelola langganan (`customer.subscription.manage`). The **Buat Langganan** dialog asks for:
   - **Paket layanan** and **Alamat pemasangan**: required. Both are search boxes (type to search, then choose).
   - **Paket layanan** holds active packages from active categories, grouped under the category name as the heading. The category that matches the customer type shows on top: a **Perorangan** customer sees **Rumahan** first, a **Perusahaan** customer sees **Bisnis**, then **Korporat**. Other categories follow their order in **Kategori Paket** (Package categories). Each option shows the package name with the package code and the price per month.
   - **Alamat pemasangan** holds the addresses of this customer. The main address is already chosen, or the only address if the customer has only one. You can still choose another. If there is no address yet, the box shows **Pelanggan ini belum punya alamat.** (This customer has no address yet.) A choice outside the customer's addresses is rejected with the message **Pilih salah satu alamat milik pelanggan ini.** (Choose one of this customer's addresses.)
   - **Tanggal tagihan (1-28)**: required. The default is 1.
   - **Biaya bulanan**, **Biaya pemasangan** and **Masa kontrak (bulan)**: optional. An empty **Biaya bulanan** uses the package price (shown as **Otomatis dari paket** (Automatic from package)). **Biaya pemasangan** has a default of 0. An empty **Masa kontrak (bulan)** uses the minimum period of the package if the package has one.
4. Click **Buat**. The system creates the subscription code and the status **Menunggu**, then the toast **Langganan dibuat.** (Subscription created.) appears. This path does not create an installation work order and does not ask for a serving POP. Create the installation work order in SPK ([section 3.3](/docs/guides/web/operations/work-orders/)) if field work is needed. Click **Batal** to close the dialog.
5. Open a subscription through the subscription code or the **Lihat** button on that page, through the subscription code or the **Kelola langganan** link in the **Langganan** tab on the customer details, through the subscription code link in ODN, or from the linked Aset Jaringan (Network assets).

   ![Subscription details page with the cards Rincian langganan and Status](~/assets/guide/web/subscriptions-show.webp)

6. The subscription details hold the card **Rincian langganan** (Subscription details) (**Pelanggan**, **Alamat pemasangan**, **POP pelayan**, **Biaya bulanan**, **Biaya pemasangan**, **Tanggal tagihan**, **Masa kontrak** (Contract period) and **Catatan** if filled in), the card **Paket** (the package name, version and its terms, see step 14), and the card **Status** (**Status**, **Tanggal aktivasi** (Activation date), **Tanggal berakhir** (End date), **Tagihan berikutnya** (Next invoice), **Berhenti pada** (Terminated on) and **Alasan berhenti** (Termination reason) if present). For a blocked subscription, the **Status** card adds **Internet**, **Alasan blokir** (Block reason) and **Diblokir oleh** (Blocked by). The panels **Alamat IP** (IP addresses) and **Port drop** appear for users with the IPAM and ODN view permissions. How to fill them is explained in Alamat IP ([section 5.4](/docs/guides/web/network/ip-addresses/)) and ODN ([section 5.5](/docs/guides/web/network/odn/)).

   On the **Rincian langganan** card, the customer name is a link to the customer details for users with Lihat pelanggan (`customer.view`).

7. Use the **Aksi langganan** (Subscription actions) card according to the status and permissions:

   | Button | Condition | Permission | Effect |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | **Aktifkan** | **Menunggu** | Aktifkan langganan (Activate subscriptions, `customer.subscription.activate`) | Becomes **Aktif**, fills in the activation date and the next invoice date |
   | **Tangguhkan** (Suspend) | **Aktif** | Tangguhkan langganan (Suspend subscriptions, `customer.subscription.suspend`) | The dialog **Tangguhkan Langganan** (Suspend Subscription) asks for **Alasan** (required, up to 500 characters). Becomes **Ditangguhkan** and the network access is isolir (internet blocked while the account is suspended) |
   | **Aktifkan kembali** (Reactivate) | **Ditangguhkan** | Aktifkan kembali langganan (Reactivate subscriptions, `customer.subscription.reactivate`) | Becomes **Aktif** and recalculates the next invoice date |
   | **Blokir internet** (Block internet) | Not yet **Berhenti** and not yet blocked | Blokir langganan (Block subscriptions, `customer.subscription.block`) | The dialog titled "Blokir internet" followed by the subscription code asks for **Alasan** (required, up to 500 characters). The status stays, billing continues, the network is isolir |
   | **Buka blokir** | Currently blocked | Blokir langganan (`customer.subscription.block`) | The dialog titled "Buka blokir" followed by the word internet and the subscription code, with **Catatan (opsional)** (Notes (optional)). If still **Ditangguhkan**, the network stays isolir |
   | **Hentikan** (Terminate) | **Aktif** or **Ditangguhkan** | Hentikan langganan (Terminate subscriptions, `customer.subscription.terminate`) | The dialog **Hentikan Langganan** (Terminate Subscription) asks for **Alasan** (required, up to 500 characters). Becomes **Berhenti**, releases the static IP and the drop port, removes the block |

   Result toasts: **Langganan diaktifkan.** (Subscription activated.), **Langganan ditangguhkan.** (Subscription suspended.), **Langganan diaktifkan kembali.** (Subscription reactivated.), **Internet langganan diblokir.** (Subscription internet blocked.), **Blokir internet langganan dibuka.** (Subscription internet unblocked.) and **Langganan dihentikan.** (Subscription terminated.)
8. Approving an installation work order with the status **Menunggu review** (Awaiting review) also activates a **Menunggu** subscription. The approval creates an installation invoice if there is a billable fee. **Kirim tagihan pemasangan otomatis** (Send installation invoice automatically) decides whether the invoice is sent straight away or stays **Draf** (Draft). **Tagihkan bahan terpakai** (Bill used materials) decides whether materials become separate lines. See SPK ([section 3.3](/docs/guides/web/operations/work-orders/)) and Tagihan ([section 7.1](/docs/guides/web/finance-and-reports/invoices/)).
9. Activation does not create a monthly invoice straight away. The Tagihan process creates a postpaid invoice per period and calculates the proration from the activation date. A **Ditangguhkan** subscription is not included in periodic invoicing. A **Berhenti** subscription can be invoiced pro rata up to the termination date.
10. A manual suspension is not lifted automatically after payment. A suspension with an arrears source can become active again after all payment conditions are met and the automatic reactivation setting is on.
11. **Blokir internet** differs from suspension. Payment does not remove the block. Use **Buka blokir** separately.
12. **Hentikan** cannot be undone. The dialog **Hentikan langganan {kode}?** (Terminate subscription {code}?) lists the consequences, only the lines that apply to that subscription: "Akun PPPoE {nama pengguna} dicabut dari RADIUS dan sesi yang tersambung diputus" (The PPPoE account {username} is revoked from RADIUS and the connected session is cut), "Alamat IP statis {alamat} kembali ke pool IPAM" (The static IP address {address} returns to the IPAM pool), "Port drop {nomor} di {ODP} dibebaskan" (Drop port {number} at {ODP} is freed), and "Tagihan bulanan dihitung sampai hari berhenti, setelah itu tidak dibuat lagi" (Monthly invoices are calculated up to the termination day, after that they are no longer created). The username, address and port are written only for users who may see network accounts, IP addresses and drop ports. The current screen does not offer an option to release the ONT at termination.
13. The lifecycle buttons appear according to the status, not according to your permissions. The server checks the permission when the button is pressed. The server also rejects actions on a status that does not fit, for example **Langganan harus berstatus Menunggu untuk diaktifkan.** (The subscription must have the status Pending to be activated.), **Langganan harus berstatus Aktif untuk ditangguhkan.** (The subscription must have the status Active to be suspended.) or **Langganan harus berstatus Ditangguhkan untuk diaktifkan kembali.** (The subscription must have the status Suspended to be reactivated.) Reload the page if the status has already changed.
14. **Ganti paket** (Change package) moves the subscription to the latest version of a package. The button is in the **Paket** card, only with Kelola langganan (`customer.subscription.manage`), and does not exist for a **Berhenti** subscription. The **Paket** card shows the package name, "Versi `<n>` sejak `<tanggal>`" (Version `<n>` since `<date>`), and the terms of that version (**Kategori**, **Harga bulanan** (Monthly price), **Harga pemasangan** (Installation price), **Profil bandwidth** (Bandwidth profile), **Profil kecepatan** (Speed profile), **Tingkat SLA** (SLA tier)). The badge **Versi terbaru tersedia** (Latest version available) appears when the package already has a newer version. Editing a package does not change existing subscriptions (see [section 4.2](/docs/guides/web/customers-and-services/service-packages/)): a subscription stays on its version until you move it.
    - Click **Ganti paket**. The window holds the search box **Paket** (active packages only, at first the current package) and **Biaya bulanan**. An empty monthly fee means the monthly price of the latest version of that package ("Otomatis dari harga paket" (Automatic from package price)). Fill in a number to use another fee.
    - Click **Pindahkan** (Move). The toast **Langganan dipindahkan ke versi terbaru paket.** (Subscription moved to the latest package version.) appears. The subscription uses the speed profile (RADIUS) and SLA tier of its new version, and **Biaya bulanan** becomes the value in that window. **Biaya pemasangan** follows the price of the new version only if the subscription is still **Menunggu**; otherwise it does not change.
    - Invoices already issued do not change. The next invoices use the new **Biaya bulanan**, with no proration between the old and the new package.
    - Rejections: "Langganan sudah memakai versi terbaru paket ini." (The subscription already uses the latest version of this package.) (the **Pindahkan** button is inactive if the chosen package is the current package and is already at the latest version), "Langganan yang sudah berhenti tidak bisa pindah paket." (A terminated subscription cannot change package.), and "Pilih paket layanan yang masih aktif." (Choose a service package that is still active.) below the **Paket** field.
    - Editing the subscription data no longer moves the package: **Ganti paket** is the only way. Every move is recorded in the audit log (`package_changed`, with the package, version and fee before and after).

## Managing network accounts

This section summarises network accounts from the subscription side. Full details are in Router NAS ([section 5.3](/docs/guides/web/network/nas-routers/)).

1. On the subscription details, click **Akses jaringan** (Network access). The button needs Lihat akun jaringan.
2. The **Akses jaringan** page shows the card **Langganan** (subscription, status, customer, speed profile, service POP and **Status yang diharapkan** (Expected status): **Aktif**, **Diisolir** (Isolated) or **Dihapus**) and, once the account exists, the card **Akun jaringan** (Network account) (service type, username, sync status, RADIUS group, **Online**, address, NAS, session start and the usage **Bulan ini** (This month)).
3. If there is no account yet and the user has Kelola akun jaringan, fill in the card **Buat akun jaringan** (Create network account):
   - **Jenis layanan** (Service type): required, **PPPoE** or **Hotspot**.
   - **Nama pengguna** (Username): required. Fill in the part before the realm with 3 to 40 lower-case letters, digits, dots, hyphens or underscores. The full name must be unique across companies. If it clashes, **Nama pengguna ini tidak tersedia.** (This username is not available.) appears.
   - **Kata sandi** (Password): optional, 6 to 64 characters without spaces. Leave it empty so that the system creates 12 characters.
4. Click **Buat akun jaringan**. The toast **Akun jaringan dibuat.** (Network account created.) appears. A password generated by the system is shown only once in the box **Kata sandi hanya tampil sekali** (The password is shown only once). Click **Salin kata sandi** (Copy password) before pressing **Tutup** (Close) or leaving the page.
5. Use the card **Ubah akun jaringan** (Edit network account) to change the service type, username or password, then click **Simpan perubahan** (Save changes). An empty password keeps the old value. A **Berhenti** subscription cannot have a network account.
6. Click **Tampilkan kata sandi** (Show password) with Tampilkan kata sandi akun jaringan. The dialog **Tampilkan kata sandi?** (Show password?) explains that the password is shown once and that the reveal is recorded in the audit log. The route is limited to 10 requests per minute.
7. Click **Sinkron ulang** (Sync again) with Kelola akun jaringan to queue a rewrite to RADIUS and a session disconnect. The toast **Sinkron ulang dijadwalkan.** (Sync again scheduled.) appears. The route is limited to 6 requests per minute.
8. If RADIUS is not configured or is not reachable, the change is still saved. The page shows **RADIUS belum dikonfigurasi untuk perusahaan ini. Perubahan disimpan tetapi tidak dikirim ke FreeRADIUS.** (RADIUS is not configured for this company. Changes are saved but not sent to FreeRADIUS.) if the company has not filled in and enabled the **RADIUS** card in **Integrasi** ([section 8.9](/docs/guides/web/administration/integrations/)), with the button **Atur di Pengaturan → Integrasi** (Set up in Settings → Integrations) for admins. Online status and usage are not available until RADIUS is available again.

## Deactivating, permanently deleting and restoring customers

1. In the list, click the **Aksi lainnya** menu on the customer row. It holds **Nonaktifkan** (Deactivate) (or **Aktifkan** (Activate) if the customer is already inactive) with the permission Ubah pelanggan (`customer.update`), and **Hapus permanen** (Delete permanently) with the permission Hapus pelanggan (`customer.delete`). **Nonaktifkan** turns off the customer's **Akun** (the **Akun** filter in the list). The customer status (**Prospek**, **Aktif**, **Nonaktif**, **Ditangguhkan**, **Berhenti**) does not change.
2. **Hapus permanen** exists only when no subscription, invoice, work order, ticket, network asset or installation history points to that customer, in any status. The numbers show in the **Nonaktifkan** dialog, for example "dipakai 1 langganan, 3 tagihan" (used by 1 subscription, 3 invoices). The dialog says "Hapus permanen `<nama>`?" (Delete `<name>` permanently?) and "Pelanggan "`<nama>`" belum pernah dipakai data lain. Penghapusan permanen tidak bisa dibatalkan." (Customer "`<name>`" has never been used by other data. Permanent deletion cannot be undone.) The customer's addresses and contacts are deleted with it. The toast **Pelanggan dihapus permanen.** (Customer permanently deleted.) appears.
3. The toast **Pelanggan dinonaktifkan.** (Customer deactivated.) or **Pelanggan diaktifkan.** (Customer activated.) appears after **Nonaktifkan** or **Aktifkan**. A **Berhenti** customer can still be deactivated.
4. The **Status** filter has **Dihapus** (Deleted) and **Semua, termasuk dihapus** (All, including deleted). A deleted customer row has a **Pulihkan** (Restore) menu (toast **Pelanggan dipulihkan.** (Customer restored.)).

## Common errors

- The **Buat Pelanggan** button does not appear for the built-in staff role because onboarding also needs Kelola langganan. Use an admin or manager, or change the role permissions.
- The message **email sudah digunakan.** (the e-mail is already used.) means the e-mail is already used by any user account. Check Pengguna ([section 8.5](/docs/guides/web/administration/users/)) or use another e-mail.
- An address error appears when the coordinates are not yet chosen (for example **lintang alamat wajib diisi.** (the address latitude is required.)), the area hierarchy does not match (**Provinsi, kota/kabupaten, kecamatan, dan kelurahan/desa tidak saling cocok.** (Province, city/regency, district and village do not match.)), or the number of markers is wrong (**Pilih tepat satu alamat utama.** (Choose exactly one main address.) or **Pilih tepat satu alamat pemasangan.** (Choose exactly one installation address.)). Complete the map and choose one card for each marker. Contacts have a similar message, **Pilih tepat satu kontak utama.** (Choose exactly one main contact.)
- The address label must be unique for the customer, including the label of an address that has been deleted. The form does not check this beforehand. Use a different label for home, office or installation point.
- Creating a **Perusahaan** fails without **NPWP** and **Narahubung** (messages **NPWP wajib diisi.** (NPWP is required.) and **narahubung wajib diisi.** (the contact person is required.)). Fill in both before saving.
- An ODP port that is already used or is outside the serving POP rejects the whole onboarding with a message such as **Port ini sudah dipakai.** (This port is already used.) or **ODP ini tidak berada di bawah POP layanan langganan** (This ODP is not under the subscription's service POP). Choose a free port under the correct POP.
- The onboarding toast says "Tautan akses akan dikirim ke &lt;email&gt;." only when **Akun aktif** is on. If it is off, the toast does not mention a link and the system sends no e-mail. Activate the account, then use **Kirim ulang link akses**.
- Changing the e-mail or **Akun aktif** on a customer does not change the user account. Update the account in Pengguna ([section 8.5](/docs/guides/web/administration/users/)).
- A fourth **Kirim ulang link akses** request within one minute produces HTTP 429. Wait until the one-minute window has passed.
- The subscription lifecycle buttons follow the status, but the server still checks permissions. An **Akses ditolak** page (code 403) means the role does not hold the permission for that action.
- An address used by a subscription cannot be deleted. The **Hapus alamat** dialog writes the reason in red text, for example "Alamat masih dipakai 1 langganan, jadi tidak bisa dihapus." Choose another address on the matching subscription before trying again.
- A customer who already has a subscription, invoice, work order, ticket, asset or installation history, in any status including **Berhenti**, cannot be permanently deleted. The dialog says, for example, "Pelanggan masih dipakai 1 langganan, 3 tagihan. Nonaktifkan saja." (The customer is still used by 1 subscription, 3 invoices. Just deactivate.) Use **Nonaktifkan** ([section 1.5](/docs/guides/web/introduction/know-the-screen/)). An inactive customer does not appear in new choices and all its history stays intact.
- The message "Tanggal kunjungan tidak boleh sebelum hari ini." (The visit date must not be before today.) below **Tanggal kunjungan**: choose today or later, or empty that field.
- The message "Kebijakan perusahaan melarang menugaskan diri sendiri." (Company policy forbids assigning yourself.) below **Teknisi**: your company forbids assignment to your own account (see Penugasan, [section 3.1](/docs/guides/web/operations/dispatch/)), yet the chosen technician is you. Choose another technician, or empty that field and assign later. The customer is not created while this message shows.
- The **Dokumen** tab has no upload form and no **Hapus** button: both exist only with Ubah pelanggan (`customer.update`). The **Unggah** button is inactive until **Jenis dokumen** and **Berkas** are chosen. A file that is not PDF, JPG or PNG, or is larger than 10 MB, is rejected. If the **Jenis dokumen** options are empty, there is no active document type for customers yet. Create one in **Jenis Dokumen** ([section 8.8](/docs/guides/web/administration/documents/)).
