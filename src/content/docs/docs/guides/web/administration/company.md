---
title: "8.1 Company (Perusahaan)"
description: "Edit the active company's profile, logo and brand colour, and set invoicing, suspension, map, service hours and WhatsApp options."
---

This menu holds the identity of the active company and its operational settings. Open it when you need to change the logo, contact details (phone, WhatsApp, service hours), the brand colour for the apps, the tax rate, the invoice due date, automatic suspension, the technician check-in distance, the service hours schedule, the default map region or WhatsApp notifications. A parent company also opens this menu to request a subsidiary.

Roles that can use it: admin. Permissions: `company.manage`. Managers and staff do not hold this permission, so the **Perusahaan** (Company) item does not appear in their sidebar and the screen address is refused. Changes apply to the active company; to switch company, see [section 1.6](/docs/guides/web/introduction/switch-company/).

## Opening and editing the profile

1. In the sidebar, open the **Administrasi** (Administration) group, then click **Perusahaan**. The **Perusahaan** screen opens with the profile form and a **Pengaturan** (Settings) button at the top right. The company name is the title of the form card.

   ![Company screen: profile form with the Name, Code, Logo, Phone, WhatsApp, Email, Website, Address, Service hours and Brand colour fields](~/assets/guide/web/company-profile.webp)

2. Fill in the form:
   - **Nama** (Name): required, at most 255 characters.
   - **Kode** (Code): read-only (it cannot be changed). The platform operator sets the code when the company is created, and it is used to confirm risky actions. Example in the demo: `DEMO`.
   - **Logo**: optional. Drag a file onto the box or click **telusuri** (browse). Use JPG, PNG or WebP, 10 MB at most. The file is converted to WebP and reduced if its longest side is more than 512 pixels. The on-screen hint also mentions SVG, but the server's `image` rule does not allow SVG, so do not use it. A new logo replaces the old one; without a new file, the old logo stays.
   - **Telepon** (Phone): optional, at most 50 characters. Only the digits 0 to 9 and the characters `(`, `)`, `+`, `-`. Other characters are removed as you type.
   - **WhatsApp**: optional, at most 50 characters, with the same characters as **Telepon**. Hint below the field: "Nomor tujuan tombol WhatsApp di aplikasi pelanggan." (The number the WhatsApp button in the customer app dials.) Write it with the country code, for example `6281234567890`. The app replaces a leading 0 with 62 when it opens WhatsApp. This is the ISP's contact number for customers, not the automatic message setting in **Pengaturan** (section below).
   - **Email**: optional, a valid email address, at most 255 characters.
   - **Situs web** (Website): optional, a full URL starting with `https://`, at most 255 characters.
   - **Alamat** (Address): optional, at most 1000 characters.
   - **Jam layanan** (Service hours): optional, free text of at most 120 characters. Example in the field: "Senin sampai Sabtu, 08.00 sampai 17.00" (Monday to Saturday, 08.00 to 17.00). Hint below the field: "Tampil di aplikasi pelanggan sebagai jam dukungan." (Shown in the customer app as support hours.) This field is text for customers only. It does not change **Jam tenang mulai** (Quiet hours start) and **Jam tenang selesai** (Quiet hours end) in the WhatsApp settings, and it does not calculate ticket deadlines. The daily opening hours schedule that calculates deadlines is in the **Jam layanan** card in **Pengaturan** (section below). Keep the two the same.
   - **Warna merek** (Brand colour): optional. Pick one of the eight colour dots (**Biru** (Blue) `#2563EB`, **Hijau tosca** (Teal) `#0F766E`, **Ungu** (Purple) `#7C3AED`, **Merah muda** (Pink) `#DB2777`, **Jingga** (Orange) `#EA580C`, **Hijau** (Green) `#16A34A`, **Biru muda** (Light blue) `#0891B2`, **Abu kebiruan** (Slate grey) `#334155`), or type a **Kode warna** (Colour code) in the format `#RRGGBB`. Picking a dot fills **Kode warna**, and typing a code equal to one of the dots selects that dot. Clear **Kode warna** to remove the brand colour. The customer and technician apps use this colour for the main card, icons and menu markers. Without a brand colour, the apps use the Inbils blue `#2563EB`.
     - Next to the field, the **Contoh teks putih** (White text sample) box shows the colour with white text, followed by the text "Kontras N:1" (Contrast N:1) (for example "Kontras 5,17:1. Teks putih terbaca jelas." (Contrast 5.17:1. White text is easy to read.)). A ratio below 4.5:1 shows the yellow warning "Di bawah 4,5:1, teks putih sulit dibaca. Pilih warna lebih gelap." (Below 4.5:1, white text is hard to read. Pick a darker colour.) Of the eight ready-made colours, **Jingga** (3.56:1), **Hijau** (3.30:1) and **Biru muda** (3.68:1) trigger that warning. The warning is only advice; you can still save.
3. Click **Simpan** (Save). While the logo uploads, the **Mengunggah logo** (Uploading logo) bar shows progress. After success, the toast "Profil perusahaan diperbarui." (Company profile updated.) appears, and the name and logo in the sidebar header change too.
4. The **Anak perusahaan** (Subsidiaries) card appears below the form when your company is a parent company (not a subsidiary of another company) and you hold `company.manage`. The platform operator creates subsidiaries, so here you only send a request:
   - Click **Minta anak perusahaan** (Request a subsidiary). A dialog with the same title opens.
   - Fill in **Nama** (required, 3 to 100 characters), **Kode usulan** (Proposed code) (optional, letters, digits, `_` and `-`, at most 50 characters) and **Catatan** (Notes) (optional, at most 500 characters, for example the branch or region meant). **Kode usulan** is filled from the name until you type a code of your own. The operator changes the code if it is already used, so the system does not check the code here.
   - Click **Kirim permintaan** (Send request). The toast "Permintaan anak perusahaan dikirim ke operator." (Subsidiary request sent to the operator.) appears, and the platform operator receives an email.
   - While the request is not finished, the **Minta anak perusahaan** button disappears and the card says "Permintaan {nama} sedang ditinjau operator." ({name} request is being reviewed by the operator.) followed by the proposed code, the notes and the time sent. One company can have only one open request. The message "Masih ada permintaan anak perusahaan yang ditinjau operator. Tunggu sampai selesai sebelum mengajukan lagi." (A subsidiary request is still being reviewed by the operator. Wait until it is finished before submitting again.) appears if another request was submitted first.
   - The operator creates the company, and the request is completed. If the operator rejects it, the requester receives the email "Permintaan anak perusahaan ditolak" (Subsidiary request rejected) with the reason. Adjust the request, then submit it again from this screen.

   ![Subsidiaries card on the Company screen, with a note that the platform operator creates subsidiaries and the Request a subsidiary button](~/assets/guide/web/company-profile-subsidiary.webp)

   ![Request a subsidiary dialog with the Name, Proposed code and Notes fields, and the Send request button](~/assets/guide/web/company-subsidiary-request-dialog.webp)

**Profile and apps.** The customer app shows **Telepon**, **WhatsApp**, **Email**, **Situs web** and **Jam layanan** in the card "Hubungi {nama perusahaan}" (Contact {company name}) on the **Akun** (Account) tab, and in the **Hubungi ISP** (Contact your ISP) sheet on the Home screen. Tapping **WhatsApp** opens wa.me, **Telepon** opens the dialler, **Email** opens the email app, and **Situs web** opens the browser. **Jam layanan** appears as text. Empty fields do not appear. If all of them are empty, the **Hubungi ISP** sheet directs the customer to the **Bantuan** (Help) tab to create a ticket. The technician app uses the company name and logo in the Home header. **Warna merek** is used by both apps. The apps read all these fields from the server when they load the account data, so a change is visible only after the app reloads.

## Opening and editing the settings

1. On the **Perusahaan** screen, click the **Pengaturan** button at the top right. The **Pengaturan Perusahaan** (Company settings) screen opens. The **Profil perusahaan** (Company profile) button on that screen takes you back. Both are ordinary buttons, not tabs. The screen is divided into the cards **Tagihan** (Invoices), **Penangguhan otomatis** (Automatic suspension), **Lapangan** (Field), **Format tanggal** (Date format), **Peta** (Map), **Jam layanan**, **WhatsApp** and **Keamanan** (Security). The section navigation (on a phone, a row of chips you can swipe above the screen; on a wide screen, a list on the left) jumps to the card and stays visible as you scroll.

   ![Company settings screen: title, the Company profile button and the Invoices settings card](~/assets/guide/web/company-settings.webp)

2. Change the invoice, suspension and field settings cards. Each setting has a name, a unit where relevant, and one sentence of explanation. The **Tagihan**, **Penangguhan otomatis** and **Lapangan** cards always appear. Until the invoice settings have been saved once, the **Tagihan** card says "Pilih cara penagihan otomatis. Saran kami: kirim tagihan pemasangan otomatis, dan isolir langganan 7 hari setelah jatuh tempo." (Choose how invoicing is automated. Our suggestion: send installation invoices automatically, and isolir (internet blocked while the account is suspended) subscriptions 7 days after the due date.) The **Pakai saran** (Use suggestion) button only fills in the form (it turns on **Kirim tagihan pemasangan otomatis** (Send installation invoices automatically) and **Tangguhkan langganan yang menunggak** (Suspend overdue subscriptions), with a limit of 7 days). Press **Simpan** to save it. After you save once, this message no longer appears and the step **Atur penagihan otomatis** (Set up automatic invoicing) in the **Mulai dari sini** (Start here) list ([section 2.1](/docs/guides/web/dashboard/queue-cards/)) is ticked.
   - **Tarif PPN (%)** (VAT rate (%)): a number from 0 to 100, step 0.01, at most two digits after the decimal point, default 11. The tax rate on new invoice items, whatever their origin (monthly, work order (SPK) or manual).
   - **Jatuh tempo tagihan (hari)** (Invoice due date (days)): a whole number from 0 to 365, default 14. The number of days from the issue date to the due date.
   - **Rekening pembayaran** (Payment account): a multi-line text box. Its content is printed on the invoice PDF.
   - **Kirim tagihan pemasangan otomatis**: switch, default off. Off means the installation invoice is created as a **Draf** (Draft), staff with `billing.send` are notified, and a card appears on the Finance Dashboard. On means the invoice is sent immediately when the installation work order is approved.
   - **Tagihkan bahan terpakai** (Invoice materials used): switch, default off. Off means materials are included in the installation fee and do not become invoice items. On means each material is invoiced per line with the label **Bahan terpakai** (Materials used).
   - **Tangguhkan langganan yang menunggak**: switch, default off. When on, the system suspends (isolates) active subscriptions that have an unpaid invoice overdue by more than **Batas keterlambatan (hari)** (Overdue limit (days)). The check runs every day at 08:00.
   - **Batas keterlambatan (hari)**: a whole number from 1 to 60, default 7.
   - **Aktifkan kembali saat tagihan lunas** (Reactivate when the invoice is paid): switch, default on. When on, a suspended subscription becomes active again automatically after payment, as long as none of its invoices is still past the grace period. A manual suspension is never lifted automatically.
   - **Jarak check-in maksimal (meter)** (Maximum check-in distance (metres)) (**Lapangan** card): a whole number from 50 to 100000, default 500. A technician who starts a job farther than this limit from the customer's location must write a reason. Without a reason, starting the job is refused with the message "Anda berjarak {jarak} dari lokasi pelanggan. Tulis alasannya untuk melanjutkan." (You are {distance} from the customer's location. Write the reason to continue.) This limit applies only when starting a job, not when setting off. The ticket page and the work order page write the distance in the **Check-in** line, followed by "Jauh dari lokasi: {alasan}" (Far from the location: {reason}) if the technician wrote a reason.
   - **Format tanggal**: text. The pattern for writing dates on all pages, for example `d M Y` or `d/m/Y`. With `d M Y`, dates appear like 13 Okt 2026; an unrecognised pattern falls back to 13/10/2026.
   - **Format tanggal dan jam** (Date and time format): text, for example `d M Y H:i`. Screens that write a date with a time use **Format tanggal** followed by the 24-hour time `HH:mm`; this field does not change that display.
3. Other settings stored for the company, for example the currency symbol or the document number prefix from the Setup Wizard, have no field on this screen. Saving this screen does not delete or change them.
4. Scroll to the **Peta** card. This card sets where the customer, address and location maps open. A point already saved on the data still takes priority:
   - **Wilayah default** (Default region): type to search, then pick a city or regency (hint "Cari kota atau kabupaten" (Search city or regency)). Picking a region fills in **Lintang** (Latitude), **Bujur** (Longitude) and **Zoom**. The cross button in the field ("Hapus pilihan: Wilayah default" (Clear selection: Default region)) clears the choice. While the region is empty and the company profile address mentions a city, Inbils offers the chip "{Kota} (dari alamat profil)" ({City} (from the profile address)). Click the chip to select it. The city name is written with a capital at the start of each word, for example "Kota Bandung".
   - **Lintang** and **Bujur**: decimal numbers. The server rejects a latitude outside -11 to 6 with "Lintang harus di antara -11 dan 6." (Latitude must be between -11 and 6.) and a longitude outside 94 to 142 with "Bujur harus di antara 94 dan 142." (Longitude must be between 94 and 142.) One of them must not be filled in alone. Clear both to return to the whole of Indonesia.
   - **Zoom**: a whole number from 3 to 18. Hint: "3 untuk seluruh Indonesia, 12 untuk satu kota." (3 for the whole of Indonesia, 12 for one city.) Outside that range, "Zoom harus di antara 3 dan 18." (Zoom must be between 3 and 18.) appears.
   - The map below: click the map or drag the marker to fill in latitude and longitude, or click **Gunakan lokasi saya** (Use my location) if the browser allows it. A point chosen on the map or typed clears the **Wilayah default** selection.
5. Scroll to the **Jam layanan** card. This card holds the company's opening hours schedule in the company's time zone. SLA tiers whose **Hitungan tenggat** (Deadline clock) is **Jam layanan** ([section 4.5](/docs/guides/web/customers-and-services/sla-tiers/)) count only the opening hours given here:
   - Each day, Monday to Sunday, has its own box with a **Tutup** (Closed) checkbox and two time pickers, **Dari** (From) and **Sampai** (Until). Tick **Tutup** for a day off. If you untick it, the hours are filled in as 08:00 to 17:00 and can be changed. The time pickers work like **Jam tenang mulai** in the **WhatsApp** card.
   - The closing time must be later than the opening time on the same day, because service hours do not cross midnight. Errors appear below the field: "Jam buka wajib diisi." (Opening time is required.), "Jam tutup wajib diisi." (Closing time is required.), "Format jam tidak valid. Contoh: 08:30." (Invalid time format. Example: 08:30.) and "Jam tutup harus setelah jam buka." (Closing time must be after opening time.)
   - While all days are **Tutup**, the schedule counts as not filled in. The card writes the warning "Jam layanan belum diisi; tenggat dihitung 24 jam." (Service hours not filled in; deadlines are counted as 24 hours.) and SLA tiers count a full 24 hours.
   - A ticket that arrives outside opening hours starts counting at the next opening time. Only SLA tiers that count by **Jam layanan** use this schedule. The **24 jam** (24 hours) SLA tier and the targets from Ticket Categories still count day and night.
   - The card reminds you that the support hours customers read in the app are filled in separately in **Profil perusahaan**, with a link to that screen. Keep the two the same.

   ![Service hours card in Company settings: the warning that hours are not filled in and the Closed checkbox for each day from Monday to Sunday](~/assets/guide/web/company-settings-service-hours.webp)

6. Scroll to the **WhatsApp** card. This section is below the edge of the screenshot above:
   - The warning "WhatsApp belum dikonfigurasi untuk perusahaan ini." (WhatsApp is not configured for this company.) appears if the **WhatsApp** card in **Integrasi** (Integrations) ([section 8.9](/docs/guides/web/administration/integrations/)) has not been filled in and enabled. Admins see the link **Atur di Pengaturan → Integrasi** (Set up in Settings → Integrations), other users see "Minta admin mengisinya di Pengaturan → Integrasi." (Ask an admin to fill it in under Settings → Integrations.) The **Kredensial terkonfigurasi** (Credentials configured) row shows Ya (Yes) or Tidak (No) for this company. The WhatsApp account is filled in per company in **Integrasi**, not on this screen.
   - **Aktifkan notifikasi WhatsApp** (Enable WhatsApp notifications): switch, default off.
   - **Jenis pesan yang dikirim** (Message types sent): nine checkboxes, all ticked by default: **Tagihan terbit** (Invoice issued), **Pengingat jatuh tempo (H-3, H-1, H+1)** (Due date reminder (D-3, D-1, D+1)), **Pembayaran diterima** (Payment received), **Tiket dibuat** (Ticket created), **Tiket mulai ditangani** (Ticket being handled), **Tiket selesai** (Ticket completed), **Penugasan SPK ke teknisi** (Work order assigned to technician), **Jadwal kunjungan teknisi ke pelanggan** (Technician visit schedule to customer) and **Info link akses portal (tanpa link)** (Portal access link info (without link)). Only **Penugasan SPK ke teknisi** has the note **Untuk teknisi** (For technicians); the other eight have the note **Untuk pelanggan** (For customers).
   - **Jam tenang mulai** and **Jam tenang selesai**: pick the time with the time picker. Type a time such as 08:30, or click the field and choose from the list in 30-minute steps ("Pilih jam" (Pick a time)). The button "Hapus jam" (Clear time) empties the field, but both must be filled in. Times follow the company time zone, default 21:00 and 07:00. The range may cross midnight. **Tagihan terbit** messages and due date reminders wait until the quiet hours end; other message types are sent immediately. A start time equal to the end time means there are no quiet hours.
   - **Batas pesan per bulan** (Message limit per month): a whole number from 0 to 100000, default 1000. Messages above the limit are skipped. Hint: "Pesan di atas batas dilewati. Isi 0 untuk tidak mengirim apa pun." (Messages above the limit are skipped. Enter 0 to send nothing.) The field must not be empty.
   - **Pemakaian bulan ini** (Usage this month): four read-only numbers, **Terkirim** (Sent), **Diterima** (Received), **Gagal** (Failed) and **Berbayar** (Paid), for the current calendar month.

   ![Lower part of the Company settings screen with the WhatsApp card, the Quiet hours start, Quiet hours end and Message limit per month fields, the Usage this month figures, and the Save button](~/assets/guide/web/company-settings-whatsapp.webp)

   Customers receive messages only if their WhatsApp consent is recorded in the customer data ([chapter 4](/docs/guides/web/customers-and-services/customers/)).
7. Scroll to the **Keamanan** card. The **Wajib 2FA untuk admin** (Require 2FA for admins) switch sets the company policy (see [section 1.4](/docs/guides/web/introduction/two-factor-authentication/)). Admins here are users who hold `company.manage`, `users.manage` or `roles.manage`. You can turn it on only if 2FA on your own account is already active. If not, Inbils writes "Aktifkan 2FA di profil Anda dulu sebelum mewajibkannya." (Enable 2FA on your profile first before requiring it.)
8. Click **Simpan** in the bar at the bottom of the screen. The bar stays visible as you scroll. While there are unsaved changes, the bar says "Ada perubahan yang belum disimpan" (There are unsaved changes). The toast "Pengaturan perusahaan diperbarui." (Company settings updated.) appears. The new values are used by the next processes: invoices created afterwards, the next suspension check, WhatsApp messages sent afterwards, new ticket deadlines and the next job start.

Currency, time zone and the app's Play Store address have no field on these two screens.

## Common errors

- Logo rejected: a red message appears below the **Logo** box, for example "logo harus berupa gambar." (the logo must be an image.) if the file is SVG or not an image, and "Ukuran logo tidak boleh lebih dari 10240 kilobita." (The logo size must not be more than 10240 kilobytes.) if it is over 10 MB. The toast "Data gagal disimpan. Periksa input." (Data could not be saved. Check the input.) also appears. Use JPG, PNG or WebP, reduce the size if needed, then upload again.
- **Situs web** rejected with "situs web harus berupa URL yang valid." (the website must be a valid URL.): write the full URL, for example starting with `https://`.
- **WhatsApp** rejected with "Nomor WhatsApp hanya boleh berisi angka dan karakter ( ) + -." (The WhatsApp number may contain only digits and the characters ( ) + -.), **Warna merek** rejected with "Warna merek harus berupa kode warna heksadesimal seperti #2563EB." (The brand colour must be a hexadecimal colour code such as #2563EB.), and **Jam layanan** rejected if it is more than 120 characters. Rewrite the field in the format above, or clear it.
- The contrast warning below **Warna merek** (yellow, reading "Di bawah 4,5:1, teks putih sulit dibaca. Pilih warna lebih gelap.") does not block **Simpan**. Pick a darker colour if you want white text on it to be easy to read.
- A number box on the **Tagihan** card that you clear is saved as empty, and the system then uses its default (due date 14 days, the system default VAT rate). **Batas keterlambatan (hari)**, **Jarak check-in maksimal (meter)**, **Batas pesan per bulan**, **Jam tenang mulai** and **Jam tenang selesai** must not be empty: a message such as "hari tunggakan penangguhan otomatis wajib diisi." (the automatic suspension arrears days are required.) or "Jarak check-in wajib diisi." (The check-in distance is required.) appears below the field.
- The server checks number ranges and writes the message below the field, because the form does not use browser warning bubbles. **Tarif PPN (%)** is rejected with "Tarif PPN paling tinggi 100%." (The VAT rate is 100% at most.), "Tarif PPN tidak boleh negatif." (The VAT rate must not be negative.), "Tarif PPN paling banyak dua angka di belakang koma." (The VAT rate has at most two digits after the decimal point.) or "Tarif PPN harus berupa angka." (The VAT rate must be a number.). **Jatuh tempo tagihan (hari)** is rejected with "Jatuh tempo paling lama 365 hari." (The due date is 365 days at most.), "Jatuh tempo tidak boleh negatif." (The due date must not be negative.) or "Jatuh tempo harus berupa bilangan bulat, dalam hari." (The due date must be a whole number, in days.). **Jarak check-in maksimal (meter)** is rejected with "Jarak check-in paling dekat 50 meter." (The check-in distance is 50 metres at the least.) or "Jarak check-in paling jauh 100.000 meter." (The check-in distance is 100,000 metres at the most.). Nothing is saved until all fields pass.
- The warning "Jam layanan belum diisi; tenggat dihitung 24 jam." does not block **Simpan**. It means SLA tiers that count by **Jam layanan** still count 24 hours, until at least one day has opening hours.
- WhatsApp messages are not sent although the switch is on: check **Kredensial terkonfigurasi**, the message type checkboxes, quiet hours, **Batas pesan per bulan** and the WhatsApp consent in the customer data.
