---
title: "3.2 Approvals (Persetujuan)"
description: "Use the approvals inbox to review work orders and item requests, then approve or reject them with a reason."
---

**Persetujuan** (Approvals) is one inbox for work orders (SPK) that await review and item requests that await approval. Open it when the number next to **Persetujuan** in the sidebar goes up.

Roles that can use it: admin and manager in the built-in roles. Permissions: `spk.approve` and `spk.reject` for work orders, `inventory.request.approve` and `inventory.request.reject` for item requests. The menu appears when you hold any of the four. The **Setujui** (Approve) button needs the approve permission and the **Tolak** (Reject) button needs the reject permission.

## Opening and reading the queue

1. In the sidebar, open the **Operasional** (Operations) group, then click **Persetujuan**. The number next to the menu is the count of items that wait for you.

   ![The Approvals page with two summary chips, the queue tabs, the filters, and the queue table](~/assets/guide/web/approvals.webp)

2. Know what does not appear. The queue holds only the items you may decide on (the maker-checker rule). Work orders that you created or that are assigned to you, and item requests that you submitted, do not appear and are not counted. This rule also applies to admins.
3. Read the two chips above the list. These chips only show numbers and cannot be clicked.
   - The **Menunggu saya** (Waiting for me) chip counts work orders with the status **Menunggu review** (Awaiting review) and item requests with the status **Menunggu persetujuan** (Awaiting approval) that you may decide on. The number is the same as the one in the sidebar.
   - The **Sudah diproses** (Already processed) chip counts work orders **Selesai** (Completed) or **Dikembalikan** (Returned) and item requests that are already **Disetujui** (Approved), **Disiapkan** (Prepared), **Siap diambil** (Ready for pickup), **Diserahkan** (Handed over), or **Ditolak** (Rejected). The count covers anyone's decisions, except items that belong to you.
4. When you hold both an SPK permission and an item request permission, the tabs **Semua antrean** (All queues), **SPK**, and **Permintaan barang** (Item requests) appear above the filters. Choosing a tab limits the list and the chip numbers to that type. With only one kind of permission, the tabs do not appear.
5. Narrow the list with the filters:
   - The **Pencarian** (Search) box ("Cari kode, judul, atau alasan..." (Search code, title or reason...)) searches the code, title or reason. The search runs after you stop typing. This queue has no **Cari** (Search) button.
   - The **Jenis** (Type) list holds **Semua jenis** (All types) and the types present in your queue: **Instalasi** (Installation), **Perawatan** (Maintenance), **Peningkatan layanan** (Service upgrade), **Pindah lokasi** (Relocation) (work order types), and **Untuk SPK** (For work order), **Untuk tiket** (For ticket), **Tanpa rujukan** (No reference) (the origin of an item request).
   - The **Pemohon** (Requester) field holds the creators of the items in your queue ("Semua pemohon" (All requesters) when empty). Type to search, then choose.
   - The **Prioritas** (Priority) list holds **Semua prioritas** (All priorities), **Rendah** (Low), **Sedang** (Medium), **Tinggi** (High), and **Mendesak** (Urgent).
   - The **Reset filter** (Reset filters) button appears when a filter is active. This queue has no date filter.
6. Read the table columns: **Kode** (Code) (the code as a link to the details when you may open them, with a **SPK** or **Permintaan barang** badge), **Judul dan dampak** (Title and impact) (the title, then the type and the first two items, each with its quantity in its own unit, for example "Kabel Drop 18 m, Konektor 2 pcs (+1)"), **Status dan prioritas** (Status and priority) (two badges: **Menunggu review** for a work order, **Menunggu persetujuan** for an item request; the priority of an item request is always **Sedang**), **Pemohon** (the creator of the work order or the submitter of the request), **Waktu dan jadwal** (Time and schedule) ("Diajukan" (Submitted) with the date and the age of the item, then "Jadwal" (Schedule) for a work order or "Dibutuhkan" (Needed) for an item request), and **Tindakan** (Actions). For a work order, the "Diajukan" date is the date the work order was created. Quantities in different units are not added together, so the table shows no total. On a narrow screen, each item appears as a card with the same **Dampak material** (Material impact) row and "{n} jenis barang" ({n} item types). The columns **Kode**, **Judul dan dampak**, **Status dan prioritas**, and **Waktu dan jadwal** can be sorted.
7. Without data, the table shows "Tidak ada persetujuan yang menunggu" (No approvals waiting) and "Semua antrean sudah diproses. Item baru muncul di sini setelah diajukan." (All queues are processed. New items appear here after they are submitted.). When a filter is active, the table shows "Tidak ada persetujuan yang cocok" (No matching approvals) with a **Reset filter** button.

## Reviewing, approving or rejecting

1. In the **Tindakan** column, click **Tinjau** (Review). The **Tinjau SPK** (Review work order) or **Tinjau permintaan barang** (Review item request) panel, followed by the item code, opens on the right.
2. Read the panel. The top holds the **SPK** or **Permintaan barang** badge, then the status, priority and type. The **Informasi SPK** (Work order information) or **Informasi permintaan barang** (Item request information) card holds the title, **Pemohon**, **Status**, **Pengajuan** (Submission) (age), **Tanggal pengajuan** (Submission date), and **Jadwal pelaksanaan** (Execution schedule) (work order) or **Tanggal dibutuhkan** (Date needed) (item request). The **Dampak material dan penggunaan barang** (Material impact and item use) card holds **Jenis item** (Item types) (the number of different items) and one row per item with its quantity in that item's unit, for example "Kabel Drop 18 m". Quantities in different units are not added together, so the panel shows no total quantity. The cards below load when the panel opens, with a grey skeleton while waiting. For a work order, the **Bukti pekerjaan** (Work evidence) card shows the **Teknisi** (Technician) name, the **Check-in** result (distance from the location, and the technician's reason when they started far from the location) and up to three evidence photos. For an item request, the **Barang diminta** (Items requested) card shows each item with the requested quantity and "Tersedia: n" (Available: n) in the source warehouse. When loading fails, the card shows "Rincian belum bisa dimuat" (Details could not be loaded) with a **Coba lagi** (Try again) button, and you can still decide. Click **Lihat detail** (View details) to open the work order page ([section 3.3](/docs/guides/web/operations/work-orders/)) or the item request page ([section 6.1](/docs/guides/web/inventory/item-requests/)) in the same tab. The **Tutup** (Close) button closes the panel.
3. To approve, click **Setujui**. The **Setujui pengajuan {kode}?** (Approve submission {code}?) dialog opens. For a work order, the dialog holds a list of impacts, for example "Pelanggan ... dan langganan ... menjadi aktif." (The customer ... and subscription ... become active.), "Tagihan pemasangan Rp ... dibuat dan langsung dikirim ke pelanggan." (The installation invoice Rp ... is created and sent to the customer at once.) (or "... dibuat sebagai draf. Kirim dari halaman tagihan." (... created as a draft. Send it from the invoices page.)), "Akun PPPoE dibuat dari kode pelanggan ..." (The PPPoE account is created from customer code ...) and "Material terpakai dikurangi dari stok: n barang." (Used material is deducted from stock: n items.) Read that list, then click **Setujui** in the dialog. For an item request you can first fill in **Catatan persetujuan (opsional)** (Approval note (optional)), 1000 characters at most. This inbox approves the quantity as requested. To approve part of the quantity, open **Lihat detail** and approve there.
4. To reject, click **Tolak**. The **Konfirmasi penolakan SPK** (Confirm work order rejection) block (or **Konfirmasi penolakan permintaan barang** (Confirm item request rejection)) appears. Fill in **Alasan penolakan** (Rejection reason) (required, 500 characters at most, the counter "n/500 karakter" (n/500 characters) appears in that block). An item request needs at least 3 characters. Click **Konfirmasi tolak** (Confirm rejection), or **Batal** (Cancel) to go back to the review. The reason is saved in the audit history and passed on to the requester.
5. After success, the panel closes, the item leaves the queue, and a toast appears: "SPK disetujui." (Work order approved.) or "SPK ditolak." (Work order rejected.) for a work order, "Permintaan barang disetujui dan stok berhasil direservasi." (Item request approved and stock reserved successfully.) or "Permintaan barang telah ditolak." (The item request has been rejected.) for an item request.
6. When the server rejects the action, the red **Gagal memproses tindakan** (Failed to process the action) alert holds its message, the **Setujui** and **Tolak** buttons are locked, and the **Muat ulang antrean** (Reload queue) button appears. Click that button. The panel closes when someone else has already processed the item, or stays open with the latest data.

Result of each decision:

- Work order approved: status **Selesai**. The system uses the material stock recorded on the work order, installs serialised assets on installation work orders, and activates the customer subscription that is still **Menunggu** (Pending).
- Work order rejected: status **Dikembalikan**. Stock does not change, because a work order does not reserve stock. The reason is saved on the work order and in the audit history. The work order appears again in **Penugasan** (Dispatch) ([section 3.1](/docs/guides/web/operations/dispatch/)) to be reassigned.
- Item request approved: status **Disetujui**, stock is reserved in the source warehouse, and the requester receives a bell notification and a push notification. The warehouse then prepares and hands over the items ([section 6.1](/docs/guides/web/inventory/item-requests/)).
- Item request rejected: status **Ditolak**, and the requester receives a bell and push notification with the reason.

## Common errors

- The queue is empty although work orders or item requests are waiting: items that you created, submitted, or that are assigned to you do not appear for you, even for admins. Ask another authorised user to decide on them.
- Approving or rejecting your own item through the details page is still refused by the server, with the message "Pembuat SPK tidak boleh menyetujui SPK sendiri." (The work order creator must not approve their own work order.), "Teknisi yang ditugaskan tidak boleh menyetujui SPK sendiri." (The assigned technician must not approve their own work order.), or "Maker-checker: pemohon tidak boleh menyetujui permintaan sendiri." (Maker-checker: the requester must not approve their own request.) For rejection, the sentence uses the word "menolak" (reject) ("Pembuat SPK tidak boleh menolak SPK sendiri.", "Teknisi yang ditugaskan tidak boleh menolak SPK sendiri.", "Maker-checker: pemohon tidak boleh menolak permintaan sendiri."). The work order page shows only the toast "Data gagal disimpan. Periksa input." (Data could not be saved. Check the input.) for this refusal ([section 3.3](/docs/guides/web/operations/work-orders/)).
- The messages "Alasan penolakan wajib diisi." (The rejection reason is required.), "Alasan penolakan minimal 3 karakter." (The rejection reason must be at least 3 characters.) (item request), or "Alasan penolakan maksimal 500 karakter." (The rejection reason must be at most 500 characters.): adjust the **Alasan penolakan** field, then click **Konfirmasi tolak**.
- The **Gagal memproses tindakan** alert with the message "SPK harus berstatus menunggu review untuk bisa disetujui." (The work order must be awaiting review to be approved.) (for rejection: "... untuk bisa ditolak."), "Permintaan barang bukan berstatus menunggu persetujuan." (The item request is not awaiting approval.) or "Hanya permintaan berstatus menunggu persetujuan yang dapat ditolak." (Only requests awaiting approval can be rejected.): another user has already processed the item. Click **Muat ulang antrean**.
- Approving a work order fails with the message "SPK belum bisa disetujui sampai seluruh retur material berstatus direkonsiliasi atau diselesaikan." (The work order cannot be approved until all material returns are reconciled or resolved.): finish the material returns of that work order in Pengembalian Barang (Material returns) ([section 6.8](/docs/guides/web/inventory/material-returns/)) until they are **Diterima** (Received) or **Selisih diselesaikan** (Difference resolved), click **Muat ulang antrean**, then approve again.
- Approving a work order fails with a message like "Stok Kabel Fiber Optic di POP Bandung tidak cukup: tersedia 0 m, diminta 18 m." (Stock of Kabel Fiber Optic at POP Bandung is not enough: available 0 m, requested 18 m.): the product stock at the named location is less than the **Terpakai** (Used) quantity. Add stock ([section 6.5](/docs/guides/web/inventory/stock/)), then approve again.
- Approving an item request fails with the message `Stok {nama barang} di {nama gudang} tidak cukup: tersedia {tersedia} {satuan}, diminta {diminta} {satuan}.`: the source warehouse stock is too low. Add stock ([section 6.5](/docs/guides/web/inventory/stock/)), or approve part of the quantity from **Lihat detail**.
- Approving an item request fails with the message "Lokasi gudang sumber wajib ditentukan untuk reservasi." (The source warehouse location must be set for the reservation.): the request was saved without a warehouse, and this inbox has no warehouse choice. Open the request through **Lihat detail** ([section 6.1](/docs/guides/web/inventory/item-requests/)). The approve dialog on that page also has no warehouse choice yet, so a request like this cannot be approved from the web yet.
