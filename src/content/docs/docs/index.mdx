Inbils is multi-tenant ISP management software: customers and subscriptions, RADIUS and MikroTik, work orders for technicians, inventory, billing with Midtrans and ticketing with SLA, with two Android apps. These pages document the web app, the operator panel and the apps, and explain how the system is built and run. They come in English and Indonesian: switch the language in the header.
